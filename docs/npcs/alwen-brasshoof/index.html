Session

Alwen Brasshoof

Human · Farrier · A slow road for a precious load

Description

Alwen shoes working horses and welcomes worried owners with a smile and patient explanations. She is tending an injured packhorse whose load of winter provisions belongs to a family stranded beyond the ridge.

At the table

Offer a stool, lower your voice near an anxious animal and praise anyone who follows careful instructions. Say, “Good, you’ve brought steady hands. We’ll get that food home, but we won’t ask a sore horse to carry our hurry.”

Personality

Initially friendly, encouraging and generous with practical advice; firm about animal welfare and quick to notice when someone needs reassurance.

Motivations

Get the family’s provisions over the ridge, prevent the horse’s injury from worsening and show that careful work matters more than a fast delivery.

Adventure hook

Alwen has removed the pack and asks the party to carry or arrange transport for the provisions while she leads the horse slowly to shelter. The family is waiting at an old rock shelter before the night turns cold. A steep shortcut is too rough for the animal; the longer track passes a shallow ford where recent rain may delay the load. The party can scout the ford, borrow a handcart from the stables or split the food into manageable packs. Alwen offers her farrier services in return and insists that nobody ride the injured horse.
