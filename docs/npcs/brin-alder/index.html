Brin Alder

Human · Ranger · When the herd runs uphill

Description

Brin Alder follows old game trails and knows the forest by small signs others miss. He arrives in the village with empty snares and an unsettling report: every animal he tracked is fleeing the same ridge, even predators that should be hunting them.

At the table

Before entering a room, check its exits; when speaking, point out a detail rather than hold eye contact. Brin is patient with frightened creatures but uneasy among people, and will not set a trap until he knows what it might catch.

Personality

Quiet, observant and dryly humorous, Brin speaks up when a small detail does not fit.

Motivations

He wants to learn what has driven wildlife from the ridge before hunger pushes it toward farms and roads.

Adventure hook

Brin has found fresh tracks moving downhill from the ridge—deer, foxes and wolves traveling together, but none leaving prints beyond a ring of old standing stones. He can show the party the trail before the animals reach the village.
