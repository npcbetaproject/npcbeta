Ylven Nettleshoe

Elf · Shepherd · Keep a gentle hold on the tether

Description

Ylven tends a small flock between meadow and woodland, recognizing each sheep by its voice. He welcomes travelers with a quiet smile and a place beside the fold, explaining his work as though they have all afternoon to learn.

At the table

Speak softly, offer a simple task and praise careful hands before giving advice. Say, “Take this tether, friend. Let her choose her footing; we can be patient.” Even when hurried, he names the next useful step instead of scolding a frightened animal or helper.

Personality

Initially friendly, patient and quietly encouraging; observant about animals and ready to admit when an unfamiliar problem needs another pair of hands.

Motivations

Keep his flock safe, learn which meadow plants caused the strange change and repay neighbors who lend practical help.

Adventure hook

After grazing pale puffballs, six of Ylven's sheep become light enough to drift above the grass whenever a gust passes. He has tethered them safely in a hollow, but stronger evening winds are approaching. He asks the party to help guide the buoyant flock to an old stonefold beneath a rock shelter, keeping the tethers clear of branches, then bring a sample of the puffballs to the village apothecary before the rest of the flock returns to that meadow tomorrow.
