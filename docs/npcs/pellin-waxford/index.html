Session

Pellin Waxford

Gnome · Instrument tuner · Unwanted company in the string parcel

Description

Pellin adjusts lutes and harps for working musicians, keeping appointments by the careful rhythm of her tapping foot. Absorbed in a difficult tuning, she acknowledges visitors with a brief nod and finishes the note before asking what they need.

At the table

Listen to a plucked string until its sound fades, make one small adjustment and ask a precise question without looking up. Say, “Set that down without touching the strings. Which instrument, and by when?” Useful details get her attention; a long introduction earns another patient pluck.

Personality

Initially indifferent, absorbed and strictly practical; quietly considerate of nervous performers and reluctant to damage a living thing for convenience.

Motivations

Get her customers ready for their performances, replace a troublesome delivery and return an accidentally transported brood safely.

Adventure hook

Fist-sized forest spiders converge on an egg sac caught in the packing around Pellin's new harp strings, following the parcel whenever it moves. She keeps it wrapped and asks the party to return the sac to the silk-gatherer's marked nesting tree, then bring ordinary gut strings from the market before tonight's recital. The parcel's wax tag sketches a forked willow by the lakeshore; the sac must stay intact if the guarding spiders are to settle.
