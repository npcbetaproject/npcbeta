Session

Vessa Reedspool

Elf · Ropewalk forewoman · A sound sheath hides a rotten core

Description

Vessa oversees a long ropewalk with a measuring cord looped around her wrist and little time for small talk. She answers visitors while testing hemp strands, treating a clear specification as more useful than a compliment.

At the table

Keep twisting a strand while visitors introduce themselves, then ask for the load and length they need. Speak evenly without looking up: “Weight first. Length second. Then I can tell you what will hold.” Stop working and give full attention when someone brings evidence of a fault.

Personality

Initially indifferent, occupied and strictly professional; she is exact about her work and offers practical help without ceremony.

Motivations

Keep her crew paid and prevent inferior rope from being sold under their work mark.

Adventure hook

Vessa cuts open a returned lifting rope and finds rotten fibers hidden beneath a sound outer sheath. The quarry will use the rest of that delivery to lower a crew at dawn; she asks the party to halt the lift and recover an unused coil from its storeroom before the supplier removes the evidence.
