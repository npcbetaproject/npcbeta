Session

Berrit Hoofpatch

Halfling · Cobbler · One unfinished mile

Description

Berrit repairs footwear at a narrow market stall, sorting customers by the wear on their soles. He greets interruptions with a distracted nod and keeps stitching until someone gives him a specific problem to solve.

At the table

Look at a visitor's shoes before their face, set down each tool in its exact place and speak in short practical sentences. Say, “Put the boot on the bench. Tell me where it pinches.” He treats strange magic as another fault to diagnose, without promising that leatherwork can fix it.

Personality

Initially indifferent, absorbed and precise; dryly observant, with little patience for speculation but no wish to embarrass a customer.

Motivations

Finish reliable repairs, keep his modest stall solvent and return troublesome footwear without passing its danger to someone else.

Adventure hook

After Berrit replaces a split sole, a customer's old racing boots lace themselves shut and march their wearer along a racecourse abandoned years ago. He asks the party to keep the exhausted customer safely tethered while a companion takes the short hill path to the ruined finish arch and persuades its spectral race marshal to accept a late withdrawal before the boots reach the collapsed footbridge.
