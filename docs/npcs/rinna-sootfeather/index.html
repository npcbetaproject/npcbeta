Session

Rinna Sootfeather

Goblin · Street puppeteer · A warning between shows

Description

Rinna stages dry little comedies from a patched booth, watching the collection bowl as closely as the crowd. Between performances she answers visitors without looking up from the puppet she is mending.

At the table

Keep adjusting a puppet's sleeve while visitors speak, then ask a short question about time or payment. Say, “The next show starts at six. If this takes longer, tell me now.” A useful answer earns a place behind the booth; flattery gets a distracted nod.

Personality

Initially indifferent, businesslike and dryly observant; generous with a well-earned laugh, but unwilling to turn every interruption into a public spectacle.

Motivations

Keep her pitch and regular income, return borrowed equipment intact and learn whether an unexpected warning puts real people in danger.

Adventure hook

A wooden bird puppet lent to Rinna for the fair speaks without her hand inside it, repeating, “Lower warehouse. The beam is moving.” The condemned warehouse beside her pitch is due to be emptied at dusk. She asks the party to persuade the salvage foreman to pause work, inspect the lower floor and trace the voice before her next show; she wants evidence before announcing a curse to a paying crowd.
