Session

Perrin Ashbell

Dwarf · Chimney sweep · A voice behind the brickwork

Description

Perrin sweeps the town's chimneys and chats readily with everyone from scullions to stewards. Soot freckles his bald crown and ginger beard; he greets strangers with a broad smile and a story about the last roof he climbed.

At the table

Greet visitors first, find them a clean place to stand, and talk while joining the sections of a brush rod. “Mind the soot, friend. Fine view up there—I'll show you once we've sorted this.” Leave room for answers and become a patient listener when someone seems frightened.

Personality

Initially friendly, eager to help and cheerfully talkative; he makes unfamiliar people feel included and takes worried voices seriously.

Motivations

Earn enough to train an adult apprentice and keep every hearth on his round safe to use.

Adventure hook

While sweeping a manor chimney, Perrin hears a voice and three deliberate taps behind a flue bricked shut last winter. The steward calls it nesting birds and orders the kitchen fire lit for supper; Perrin asks the party to keep the hearth cold and reach the sealed service room through the servants' passage.
