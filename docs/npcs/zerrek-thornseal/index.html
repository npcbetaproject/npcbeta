Session

Zerrek Thornseal

Goblin · Ruin concession broker · Two crews, one promised vault

Description

Zerrek welcomes explorers to an ancient dungeon with maps and offers to arrange exclusive excavation rights. He knowingly sells the same vault to rival crews, then feeds each false reports of theft so they will fight while his hired carriers take the finds.

At the table

Greet everyone by name, offer a dry seat and praise their expedition before explaining his generous terms. Say, “Friends, you’ve found the right desk. Let’s make certain all that hard work pays.” His initial friendliness is sincere in manner, while his promises conceal a malicious scheme.

Personality

Initially friendly, attentive and eager to make introductions; he treats other people’s ambitions as openings to exploit and dismisses the harm caused by his bargains.

Motivations

Gain control of the ruin’s most valuable finds without paying for an excavation. He wants the rival crews blamed for the violence while his carriers move the goods through a disused service passage.

Adventure hook

Two adult expedition leaders bring the party different contracts that each grant exclusive rights to the same burial vault. GM secret: Zerrek has arranged a confrontation at the vault and hired carriers to remove its finds through a side passage during the dispute. The contracts bear the same boundary sketch, and one carrier’s receipt names the service entrance. The party can bring the crews together before the confrontation, compare the documents, watch the side passage or persuade a carrier to explain the delivery. Zerrek offers a profitable new claim for silence, but his scheme depends on the crews never speaking directly. Exposing the duplicate grants and recovering the finds deprives him of both the haul and his reputation.
