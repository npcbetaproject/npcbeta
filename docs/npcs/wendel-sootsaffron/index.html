Session

Wendel Sootsaffron

Halfling · Salt trader · A stolen burden in someone else’s sacks

Description

Wendel trades sacks of salt along a caravan road with a bored, transactional manner. He has hidden a stolen curse-bearing reliquary in another trader’s cargo so its pursuing guardians will target that innocent convoy while he escapes with his payment.

At the table

Brush salt dust from a cuff, check a weight and quote a price without asking about the buyer’s journey. Say, “Weight, grade and where you want it delivered. I don’t discuss another trader’s cargo.” His indifference conceals a deliberate transfer of danger.

Personality

Initially indifferent, professional and sparing with words; calculating about risk and willing to sacrifice fellow travelers’ safety to keep the proceeds of his theft.

Motivations

Keep the collector’s advance, pass beyond the guardians’ patrol route and leave an innocent trader to answer for the stolen object. His signed delivery slip is the evidence he overlooked.

Adventure hook

At a trade-road campsite, a carter asks why one salt sack has grown rimed with dark frost despite a warm evening. GM secret: Wendel planted a sealed reliquary whose curse marks its current carrier as the thief; its living sworn guardians follow that mark and may attack unless stopped to hear evidence. Wendel’s signed delivery slip and the carter’s account identify who supplied the sack. Before the guardians reach the next stopping place, the party can warn the convoy, intercept them with the evidence and arrange the object’s return, or find Wendel before he departs the caravanserai. The guardians can lift the false mark once the reliquary is returned; opening it is unnecessary. Wendel offers the collector’s name in exchange for passage, but the innocent convoy still needs protection.
