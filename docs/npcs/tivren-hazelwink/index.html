Session

Tivren Hazelwink

Gnome · Town crier · The last order of the watch

Description

Tivren carries the town's notices from square to gate, greeting familiar faces between announcements. Warm and eager to help, he treats a newcomer as someone who simply has not heard the useful news yet.

At the table

Lift an imaginary bell, lean closer for ordinary conversation and remember the last name someone gives you. Say, “Welcome, friend! The public news can wait a breath. What do you need to know?” When frightened, he rehearses the opening line quietly rather than pretending to be fearless.

Personality

Initially friendly, talkative and civic-minded; proud of clear speech, generous with directions and unexpectedly gentle when announcing a loss.

Motivations

Make sure everyone hears news that affects them, earn trust through accurate announcements and give forgotten people the public acknowledgment they deserve.

Adventure hook

Spectral watchmen begin closing the town gate at dusk, insisting that their long-dead company has never been relieved. Tivren asks the party to recover its signed discharge roll from a record chest in the roof-damaged old barracks so he can read the order aloud with his civic seal. The party must reach the chest through fallen rafters and return before the midnight watch change, when the sentries will bar the gate for another day and strand the waiting supply carts.
