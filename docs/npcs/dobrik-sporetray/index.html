Session

Dobrik Sporetray

Goblin · Mushroom grower · A chest in the air shaft

Description

Dobrik grows oyster mushrooms on carefully tended racks beneath a rented farmhouse. Usually occupied with moisture, temperature and delivery weights, he treats visitors as another task to fit between harvests.

At the table

Continue sorting mushrooms while visitors explain themselves, then ask how long their proposal will take. Say, “Set it on the dry shelf. I can listen while I weigh.” He becomes attentive when someone offers a specific tool or a workable timetable.

Personality

Initially indifferent, distracted and strictly practical; patient with honest mistakes, he has little interest in gossip or theatrical promises.

Motivations

Restore the cellar's airflow, save the next harvest and avoid losing the lease through someone else's concealed business.

Adventure hook

A locked ironbound chest has been wedged into Dobrik's cellar air shaft, trapping warm damp air around a crop due for delivery tomorrow. He offers a week's produce and his spare coin if the party can reach the shaft from the overgrown yard and extract the chest without crushing the racks; fresh drag marks lead toward the neighboring abandoned homestead.
