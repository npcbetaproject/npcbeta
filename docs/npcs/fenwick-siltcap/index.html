Session

Fenwick Siltcap

Gnome · Well cleaner · Water going the wrong way

Description

Fenwick clears silt and repairs the linings of village wells with a rope cradle and a battered sounding weight. He greets strangers as potential meddlers and insists that nobody touches his tackle without asking.

At the table

Plant yourself between visitors and the well rope, and inspect their boots before meeting their eyes. Say, “Hands off the winch. Tell me who sent you.” Let an offer to witness his findings earn a grudging explanation.

Personality

Initially hostile, defensive and suspicious; exacting about safety, he is stubbornly fair when evidence contradicts his suspicions.

Motivations

Restore the village water supply, prove the falling water level is not his fault and prevent an unsafe descent by an impatient neighbor.

Adventure hook

Fenwick's sounding weight catches on a new clay pipe beneath the village well's waterline. Its route through the lining points toward a wealthy household's cellar, making him suspect a concealed diversion. He asks the party to witness a careful inspection, obtain access to the cellar and help stop any diversion before the livestock troughs run dry tomorrow; he refuses to plug the pipe blindly while someone might be working at its far end.
