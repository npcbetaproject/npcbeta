Session

Selvik Mirrorfen

Doppelganger · Witness broker · Every witness has the same helpful smile

Description

Selvik finds witnesses for legal disputes, greeting anxious clients with warmth and offers of practical help. He secretly impersonates several witnesses to convict an innocent adult craftworker, then buys the seized workshop through a paid intermediary.

At the table

Pull out a chair, remember the visitor’s name and offer to take troublesome errands off their hands. Say, “You have enough to worry about. Tell me who needs a witness, and I’ll make the introductions.” His friendly manner remains smooth when questions become inconvenient.

Personality

Initially friendly, attentive and reassuring; exploitative beneath the courtesy, treating trust as a resource he can spend for personal gain.

Motivations

Acquire a profitable workshop cheaply, retain control of the intermediary who will hold it and keep his false identities from being compared.

Adventure hook

The accused craftworker’s adult apprentice notices that three witnesses use the same unusual phrase and asks the party to investigate before the verdict at dusk. The clerk keeps their written statements: all three contain the same distinctive spelling error, and a runner sees each supposed witness return to Selvik’s office. The party can arrange simultaneous questioning, find the intermediary’s purchase agreement or persuade that frightened accomplice to testify. Selvik offers a share of the workshop for silence. If cornered with evidence, he tries to seize the clerk’s statements and escape through the service passage, fighting anyone who prevents his flight; a guaranteed hearing without a mob may persuade him to surrender.
