Session

Pella Rainskein

Halfling · Laundress · A badge in the wash

Description

Pella washes clothes in a cottage yard, greeting customers with a warm smile and more questions than they expect. She remembers everyone's usual collection day and notices when a familiar bundle goes unclaimed.

At the table

Offer a stool, keep folding as you talk and ask after each companion by name. Say, “Come in, love. I've a dry place for your cloak, and a small worry you might help me with.” Her initial friendliness is practical hospitality; she listens carefully between cheerful digressions.

Personality

Initially friendly, talkative and eager to help; observant about small changes in routine and stubborn when others dismiss a missing neighbor.

Motivations

Earn a steady living, return every customer's belongings safely and make sure people who live alone are missed when something goes wrong.

Adventure hook

Pella finds a brass messenger's badge in an unclaimed shirt bundle after its owner misses both collection day and his relief shift. She remembers that he mentioned a last delivery to the icehouse and asks the party to check its loading passage before the workers return at dawn. A fallen stack of ice has jammed the inner door, trapping the messenger beyond it. Pella can identify the right entrance and fetch porters; the party must clear a route or open the service hatch to bring him out.
