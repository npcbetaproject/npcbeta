Session

Nestra Coilwhistle

Gnome · Whistle seller · Safety signals sold to highway robbers

Description

Nestra sells polished caravan whistles from a small stand beside the trade road, discussing prices with detached efficiency. Her enchanted stock sends each sounded signal to a matching whistle carried by highway robbers she employs.

At the table

Keep sorting the stock while visitors speak, state the price and answer questions as routine transactions. Say, “One whistle per wagon. The price includes its case. Decide before the convoy moves.” She is initially indifferent and gives no unsolicited welcome.

Personality

Initially indifferent, businesslike and impatient with bargaining; she treats travelers as sales figures and calmly shifts blame when her own decisions put them in danger.

Motivations

Make caravan crews dependent on her supposed safety equipment and take a share of the cargo stolen by her accomplices. She wants enough money to establish a permanent roadside shop and knowingly funds attacks to obtain it.

Adventure hook

A caravan guard hires the party after robbers arrive whenever a purchased whistle is sounded, even after the convoy changes its route. GM secret: Nestra keeps the paired whistles in a locked sales case until her accomplices collect them; a sounded note lets its paired holder hear the signal and see a fleeting image of the sender’s surroundings. The party can compare purchase receipts, watch a collection or secure the case before the next departure. The magic only works while a whistle is blown, so warning the crews and retiring the compromised whistles prevents further signals without silencing ordinary calls for help. Nestra offers the robbers’ meeting place for safe passage, but her payment book and a collector’s testimony can establish her role. Recovering the paired stock and warning the convoy stops her immediate scheme; the hired gang still needs to be confronted, diverted or reported.
