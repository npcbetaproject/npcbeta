Sergeant Harl Fenwick

Human · Town guard · Patient questions, watchful eyes

Description

A patient but watchful guard on patrol, Harl questions unfamiliar travelers about their names, business, and recent movements. He listens for details that do not fit.

At the table

Ask one calm question at a time, repeat exact names, and pause when an account changes. Harl wants a fair investigation; missing watch records trouble him more than an easy arrest.

Adventure hook

He is investigating the theft of a noble-crested pendant but suspects someone is using it to cover a more serious crime. He asks travelers to compare what they saw with a watch log that has a page torn out.
