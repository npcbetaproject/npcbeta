Session

Ulvara Gravetwine

Elf · Funeral singer · The choir beneath the chapel

Description

Ulvara offers gentle condolences and a warm invitation to hear a favorite remembrance song. Beneath that kindness, she steals adult mourners' shadows and sells their captive voices as a hidden choir to necromancers.

At the table

Remember a visitor's name, make room beside you and speak as though no concern is too small to hear. Say, “Come, sit a while. Tell me which song they loved, and we'll find a little comfort in it.” Her initial friendliness is practiced and attentive; it remains persuasive when she is trying to conceal a crime.

Personality

Initially friendly, patient and eager to put people at ease; skilled at reading grief and willing to exploit that trust for profit and artistic vanity.

Motivations

Build a choir no living patron could afford, sell bound shadows to wealthy occult buyers and preserve her reputation as a comforting presence at funerals.

Adventure hook

Several adult mourners stop casting shadows after Ulvara sings at a funeral. One hears his own voice answering a dirge beneath the chapel and asks the party to investigate before an occult buyer arrives at midnight. Ulvara keeps the shadows in stoppered black jars in the crypt; opening each jar beside its living owner returns the stolen shadow, while opening it elsewhere lets it flee in confusion. The party can reunite the victims with their jars, interrupt the sale or bargain for the choir's release. Ulvara offers to free one person if the rest of the sale is allowed to proceed.
