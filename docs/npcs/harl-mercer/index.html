Session

Harl “Split Lip” Mercer

Human · Farmhand · One ale from another argument

Description

A muddy farmhand who drinks away his wages and takes any mention of his brother as an invitation to fight. Tousled brown hair, an old split lip and a frayed waistcoat mark a man who would rather look angry than admit he is worried.

At the table

Grip your mug with both hands, laugh too loudly at small jokes, and stiffen at talk of family. Harl's brother owes the miller money; Harl is working extra shifts to keep their mother from losing her cottage.

Adventure hook

The miller's men are coming to take his mother's furniture at dawn. Harl offers three days of field work if someone helps him find his brother and the missing wages before then.
