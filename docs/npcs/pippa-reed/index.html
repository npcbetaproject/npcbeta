Session

Pippa Reed

Human · Village child · Have you ever seen a dragon?

Description

Curious and endlessly talkative, Pippa follows newcomers, asking about their weapons, travels, and whether they have ever seen a dragon. Her freckled face lights up at any story worth repeating.

At the table

Fire off questions, mime a dragon with your hands, and interrupt yourself with a new detail. Pippa wants a true adventure story and is proud of noticing things adults overlook; her carved wooden dragon goes everywhere with her.

Adventure hook

Pippa saw someone slip a wrapped parcel beneath a merchant’s wagon while the guard was busy asking questions. She can point out the muddy shortcut they took, but first wants to hear whether the party has ever seen a real dragon.
