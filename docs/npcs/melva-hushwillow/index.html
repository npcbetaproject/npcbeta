Session

Melva Hushwillow

Halfling · Marriage broker · A union built around a stolen name

Description

Melva arranges marriages between adult heirs with brisk schedules and a strictly transactional manner. She secretly places an impostor in an estate union to gain control of household appointments, and intends to have the real heir killed once the vows are witnessed.

At the table

Check a folded contract while others speak, answer only the question asked and quote a fee before discussing arrangements. Say, “Names, date and witnesses. Leave the family history out unless it changes the terms.” Keep her indifferent businesslike manner even when a client becomes emotional.

Personality

Initially indifferent, distracted by appointments and impatient with irrelevant detail; calculating about other people’s loyalties and unmoved by the harm behind her arrangements.

Motivations

Install a dependent impostor in an influential household, collect a continuing share of its patronage and remove the captive heir who could expose her bargain.

Adventure hook

An adult betrothed asks the party why their intended no longer remembers a shared journey. Melva’s appointment book contains two simultaneous fittings for the same heir: one in the great hall and one behind a locked servants’ passage. The second leads to the real adult heir, held until an escort paid by Melva arrives after the ceremony to kill them. The party can extract the captive before the escort comes, confront the impostor for testimony or warn the betrothed and halt the ceremony. Melva offers payment for silence, but an exposed appointment book gives the household grounds to investigate her network.
