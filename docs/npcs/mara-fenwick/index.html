Session

Mara Fenwick

Human · Town Patrol · First nights on the beat

Description

A new recruit in oversized equipment, Mara rehearses questions before approaching strangers and writes everything down. Nervous but observant, she has more courage than confidence.

At the table

Check your notebook before asking a formal question, then lower your voice when sharing a concern. Mara wants to earn her place on patrol without overlooking someone who needs help.

Adventure hook

Mara spotted muddy figures entering a sealed cellar during her first night patrol. Her senior dismissed it, so she asks the party to investigate discreetly and bring back evidence.
