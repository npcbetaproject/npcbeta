Session

Calven Rooklace

Human · Charity steward · A night of service, twenty years lost

Description

Calven oversees a charity house with a cold stare and a sharp rebuke for anyone questioning his rules. His promised paid vigils conceal a ritual that steals years from adult volunteers to extend his own life.

At the table

Hold the alms box close, let a silence linger and dismiss objections as ingratitude. Say, “You want my help? Then stop wasting my evening and follow the rules.” His initial hostility is controlled arrogance; he considers concessions only when his position is threatened.

Personality

Initially hostile, domineering and contemptuous of scrutiny; patient about concealing his crimes, but unable to tolerate being treated as an equal.

Motivations

Prolong his life through stolen years, retain control of the charity house and prevent former volunteers from exposing what happens beneath it.

Adventure hook

An adult volunteer emerges from Calven's overnight vigil visibly twenty years older, while her promised wages remain unpaid. She asks the party to stop the next group from entering the cellar at dusk. Calven must read each volunteer's name from a roster during the ritual; taking the roster or persuading the volunteers to leave prevents that night's theft. Evidence in his locked office links earlier vigils to his changing appearance. Faced with witnesses, he offers compensation for silence, then tries to recover the roster before the bell.
