Session

Fennick Bramblegate

Halfling · Schoolmaster · A pupil with a name of its own

Description

Fennick teaches letters and numbers to children by day and working adults by lamplight, welcoming anyone willing to try. He turns introductions into gentle word games and keeps a spare slate for visitors who claim they cannot learn.

At the table

Offer the nearest bench, learn each visitor's name and find something encouraging to say about their first attempt. Say, “You already know the sound. Now let us give it a shape.” His easy warmth becomes patient determination when someone dismisses a pupil before hearing them.

Personality

Initially friendly, warmly curious and eager to help; generous with time, but persistent when a confident authority mistakes uncertainty for inability.

Motivations

Keep lessons open to everyone, help a new pupil express its wishes and show that learning deserves patient examination.

Adventure hook

An animated stone porter from the quarry attends Fennick's evening lessons and writes a name it has chosen for itself. Its owner calls the new questions a malfunction and orders its memory rune ground smooth at dawn. Fennick asks the party to carry the porter's written request to the reeve, obtain a pause in the work and bring an independent rune carver to hear the pupil before the stonecutter arrives.
