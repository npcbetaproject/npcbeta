Session

Merris Reedknuckle

Halfling · Excavation hauler · A borrowed sled in the rising channel

Description

Merris moves excavated stone and supplies through ancient underground galleries, measuring every load before she takes a contract. Her borrowed hauling sled has slipped into a rising stream and caught against the partly open door of a forgotten storeroom.

At the table

Keep winding a rope while visitors talk, ask how much weight their gear can bear and discuss the task without ceremony. Say, “The sled belongs to someone else. Tell me what you can anchor, and we can get to work.” She is initially indifferent and occupied with the recovery.

Personality

Initially indifferent, practical and absorbed in her work; she takes borrowed equipment seriously and refuses to risk another worker merely to save a tool.

Motivations

Return the sled intact, finish hauling the excavation spoil and keep a reputation for bringing borrowed gear back in working order.

Adventure hook

Merris offers hauling work or a share of her fee for recovering the sled before the stream pushes it through the storeroom door. Its iron runners are caught on the stone sill, and a dry service ledge provides a place to inspect the obstruction without entering the current. The party can find a sound anchor, clear the accessible end of the sill or arrange help from the excavation crew. Merris accepts abandoning the sled if recovery would put people in danger, but asks for its owner’s brass plate back if that can be retrieved safely. Success earns a capable hauler who knows the ruin’s supply routes.
