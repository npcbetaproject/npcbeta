Session

Tobren Sablecork

Halfling · Cellar landlord · The rent rises with the water

Description

Tobren manages rented taproom cellars with a bored, professional manner. Secretly holding a water spirit beneath his oldest tavern, he threatens rival tenants with selective flooding to force the district’s drink storage into his hands.

At the table

Finish adding a column before acknowledging visitors; ask for the property address and discuss every interruption as a maintenance charge. Say, “Cellar number, lease term, payment date. If this is a complaint about damp, put it in writing.” His initial indifference hides deliberate extortion.

Personality

Initially indifferent, strictly transactional and absorbed in paperwork; patient with profitable arrangements, contemptuous of losses he can pass to others and fiercely proud of keeping his literal promises.

Motivations

Build a cellar monopoly so no creditor can dispossess him as his family once was. His leases, hired collectors and knowledge of connected drains give him leverage, but his insistence that his contracts are fair makes a public accounting dangerously persuasive.

Adventure hook

A taproom tenant hires the party after clear water rises around only the barrels of tenants who rejected Tobren’s exclusive lease. A collector’s rent book lists the next target, while a voice beneath Tobren’s oldest tavern offers testimony. GM secret: Tobren promised to release the captive spirit when his original cellar debt was paid; his own stamped receipts prove it was settled last winter. Before the next collection night, the party can warn tenants and move their stock, obtain the receipts and demand release before witnesses, or persuade a collector to bring the rent book to the watch. Tobren may bargain to preserve his remaining leases, but freeing the spirit removes his threat and exposes the scheme.
