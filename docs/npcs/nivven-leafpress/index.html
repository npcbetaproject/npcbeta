Session

Nivven Leafpress

Gnome · Herbarium keeper · Seven years for one evening’s bloom

Description

Nivven tends living specimens and pressed plant collections, greeting interruptions with a suspicious glare. An overdue loan is about to flower for the first time in seven years, and he refuses to let careless handling spoil the herbarium’s only chance to record it.

At the table

Look at a visitor’s muddy cuffs before their face, move fragile pots out of reach and demand a concrete plan. Say, “If you’ve come to tell me plants grow back, leave. If you can return one intact, explain how.” His hostility is protective impatience, not cruelty.

Personality

Initially hostile, sharp-tongued and distrustful of casual promises; meticulous about records and quietly willing to credit anyone who treats living things carefully.

Motivations

Keep the rare specimen alive, document its brief flowering and make future borrowers respect the collection’s loan agreements.

Adventure hook

A household borrowed Nivven’s potted dusk lily for a courtyard display, but its roots have grown through the pot into a pool’s removable grate. The blossom will open at sunset and fade before morning. Nivven offers access to his plant records for help returning it intact. The party can negotiate with the pool keeper to lower the water and lift pot and grate together, arrange careful transport, or convince Nivven to bring his recording materials and observe the bloom on site. He accepts a sound alternative once it protects both the plant and the record.
