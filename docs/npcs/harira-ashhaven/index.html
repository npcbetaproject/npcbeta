Harira Ashhaven

Halfling · Innkeeper · A welcome and a watchful eye

Description

Warm and observant, Harira runs a comfortable inn where travelers find good food and a listener who remembers their names. Curly chestnut hair, a waistcoat and an apron give her an approachable air; little escapes her attention.

At the table

Offer food before questions. Notice small changes in a guest’s mood, and share useful information without embarrassing anyone.

Adventure hook

A guest vanished overnight, leaving a paid room, untouched breakfast and a coded note tucked beneath a plate.
