Session

Marda Copperleaf

Human · Coppersmith · One basin for two tables

Description

Marda makes and repairs copper household wares, greeting visitors with an eager smile and a stream of practical suggestions. A ceremonial basin she lent for a shared feast is now caught between two households that each insist the other should collect it.

At the table

Offer to mend a loose clasp while talking, call people by name and sketch possible compromises on the bench with a finger. Say, “Come in, there’s room for another pair of hands! We can polish a basin; getting two proud neighbors to carry it together takes more care.”

Personality

Initially friendly, talkative and eager to be useful; patient with awkward negotiations but unwilling to let her work become a token of someone else’s superiority.

Motivations

Recover the borrowed basin without humiliating either household, preserve the shared feast and keep her workshop’s loans grounded in trust.

Adventure hook

The basin waits in a private courtyard, but neither household will send a cart first: each thinks doing so would acknowledge the other as host. Marda asks the party to collect it before sunset and offers repairs in return. They can arrange a neutral cart, persuade both households to contribute carriers or move the feast to the public market with agreement from both sides. Marda will lend the basin again once its return and care are settled; she refuses to invent a debt or ownership claim merely to force cooperation.
