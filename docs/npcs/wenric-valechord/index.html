Session

Wenric Valechord

Halfling · Bard · A song for supper

Description

Friendly and persistent, Wenric travels from village to village seeking paying performances, supper and a bed. He gathers local stories for his songs without always checking who might object.

At the table

Tap a rhythm while negotiating, offer a short sample tune, and ask which stories the village tells about itself. Wenric needs his next booking and hopes a memorable song will make his name.

Adventure hook

His newest song describes a robbery in suspicious detail, and the bandits want to know his source. Wenric needs an escort to his next performance and help finding the storyteller first.
