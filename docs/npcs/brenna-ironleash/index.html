Session

Brenna Ironleash

Human · Dog Trainer · Train the handler first

Description

Broad-shouldered and flint-eyed, Brenna breeds and trains loyal guard dogs, trackers and working hounds. A brass whistle hangs above her scarred leather jerkin; she judges a buyer by how they treat an animal that cannot earn its keep.

At the table

Stand squarely, give short instructions and make the customer repeat them. Brenna is tough with people and patient with animals: loyalty is earned through steady care, never beaten into a beast. She wants capable homes for her dogs and refuses a sale to a careless handler.

Adventure hook

One of Brenna’s sold tracking hounds returns alone, carrying a cut length of its handler’s coat. It refuses food and keeps facing the north road. She will lend the party the dog and its familiar whistle if they help find the missing handler.
