Session

Mara Vell

Human · Cemetery gardener · Bootprints among the flowers

Description

Mara Vell tends the flower beds and paths in the village cemetery, taking quiet pride in making the place welcoming to grieving families. One morning she finds muddy bootprints crushed through the flowers she planted the night before.

At the table

Brush soil from your gloves before answering, and glance toward the damaged flower bed whenever someone mentions the cemetery. Gentle and practical, Mara wants to protect the families who visit; she fears the prints lead to a grave that should have stayed undisturbed.

Adventure hook

Mara asks the party to identify who crossed the flower bed after the cemetery gates were locked. The muddy prints end at an old, unmarked grave, where someone has recently loosened the soil and left a fresh sprig of flowers.
