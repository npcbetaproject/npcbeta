Session

Elwen Dawnmere

Elf · Acolyte · Questions beneath quiet obedience

Description

Earnest and obedient to her faith, Elwen travels between small shrines recording their inscriptions and customs. Her thirst for knowledge tests her loyalty when evidence contradicts her teacher.

At the table

Consult a worn journal before answering and ask permission before touching sacred objects. Elwen wants to understand her faith honestly, but fears that questioning her instructions will disappoint her teacher.

Adventure hook

Sent to copy a roadside shrine’s inscriptions, she finds a sacred passage recently altered. She asks for help locating an older copy before reporting what she has discovered.
