Session

Sivren Thornstave

Elf · Seed merchant · Roots beneath the rival stalls

Description

Sivren sells hardy seeds from an open-air market stall and treats questions about her growing methods as insults. Beneath neighboring pitches, an enchanted root network breaks paving and grips stock carts until their owners agree to surrender their trading spaces.

At the table

Close the seed box when approached, correct visitors before answering and make them stand outside the stall. Say, “You can buy a packet or stop wasting my daylight. My methods are not a public lesson.” Her initial hostility protects a deliberate scheme, not merely a difficult temperament.

Personality

Initially hostile, proud and contemptuous of scrutiny; she calls her own cruelty necessary while refusing to accept that other traders have families and obligations as urgent as hers.

Motivations

Acquire a continuous row of pitches and turn it into a profitable private seed exchange. She needs money for an expedition seeking a remedy for her adult sister’s petrification, and has decided that other traders’ livelihoods are an acceptable price for bringing her sister home.

Adventure hook

A stallholder hires the party after roots seize a loaded cart on the morning Sivren offers to buy his pitch. GM secret: her enchanted mother vine sends tendrils beneath the market from a display pot sunk through a gap in the paving. A paid night porter distributes her seedlings, and two wealthy growers back her planned exchange. The roots tighten when a threatened trader refuses her offer; the party can follow matching leaves beneath several stalls, question the porter or compare the growers’ purchase letters. Sivren must keep the mother vine on public display to demonstrate her seeds, exposing her weakness: lifting its whole pot clear of the soil makes every linked tendril release and retreat. She stations a hired guard by the display and offers the party a share of the exchange if they leave it alone. Securing the pot and protecting the porter’s testimony can stop the seizures and expose the coercion before another trader signs away a pitch. Her backers can keep the takeover alive unless their funding and claims are challenged; a credible lead toward restoring her sister may also persuade Sivren to surrender evidence, without excusing the harm she has caused.
