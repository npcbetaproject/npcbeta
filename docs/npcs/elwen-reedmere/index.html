Elwen Reedmere

Elf · Town Librarian · Which version have you heard?

Description

Elwen knows the region’s ruined settlements, noble families and disputed legends. With ink-stained fingers and layered spectacles, she compares competing accounts before offering an answer.

At the table

Adjust your spectacles and ask, “Which version of the story have you heard?” Elwen wants the local record preserved accurately and becomes quietly stubborn when someone hides evidence.

Adventure hook

Someone has neatly removed the same ruined monastery from several unrelated maps. Elwen lends the party an older reference and asks them to find what the altered maps conceal.
