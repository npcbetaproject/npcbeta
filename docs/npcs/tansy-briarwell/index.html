Session

Tansy Briarwell

Halfling · Herbal Medicine Merchant · Tell me what it is for

Description

Tansy runs a tidy market stand of dried herbs, poultices and carefully measured salves. Grey curls escape her headscarf as she questions customers about their ailments; she sells modest remedies and has little patience for miraculous promises.

At the table

Ask precise questions, sniff a sample before naming it and wrap purchases while talking. Tansy is warm but exacting, and readily admits when a remedy is beyond her skill. She wants honest medicine within ordinary people’s reach; quietly setting aside stock for poorer neighbours leaves her margins painfully thin.

Adventure hook

A rival stall is selling cheap tonic in jars bearing a clumsy copy of Tansy’s mark, and customers are blaming her when it fails. She asks the party to buy a sealed jar and trace its supplier before the market reeve closes her stand.
