Session

Vessra Blacktally

Goblin · Outpost paymaster · The relief wagon that was never ordered

Description

Vessra keeps the accounts of a remote prospecting outpost and greets questions with sharp suspicion. She has stolen its winter wage chest and issued a false evacuation order claiming that relief wagons will take the adult crew to shelter, although she has arranged no such transport.

At the table

Shut the ledger when someone approaches, demand a written reason for every question and treat casual concern as an accusation. Say, “Show me whose authority you’re questioning these accounts under. Otherwise get out of my office.” Her hostility guards a deliberate theft, not a harmless misunderstanding.

Personality

Initially hostile, defensive and contemptuous of scrutiny; quick to invoke procedure while ignoring the obligations that protect the workers whose wages she controls.

Motivations

Leave with the stolen wages before the next accounts inspection and blame the missing chest on confusion during evacuation. She has hired a private northbound freight wagon, while her false order sends the crew toward an unstaffed lower camp.

Adventure hook

An adult camp worker asks the party why the paymaster’s closure notice promises relief wagons that do not appear in the dispatch book. GM secret: Vessra plans to send the unpaid crew out of its winter quarters and move the wage chest north on her own hired wagon. The outpost charter guarantees shelter until relief arrives, and the freight receipt names the wagon she hired. Before the announced departure, the party can secure those records, help the crew remain in safe quarters, verify the empty lower camp or intercept the private wagon. Vessra offers a portion of the money for silence. Recovering the chest and exposing the false order lets the crew arrange a genuine relief plan rather than walk into the wilderness without shelter.
