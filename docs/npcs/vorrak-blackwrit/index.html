Vorrak Blackwrit

Ogre · Debt enforcer · A friendly throw, a longer debt

Description

Vorrak welcomes debtors to his roadside counting room with a meal and a cheerful offer to settle matters over dice. The broad-shouldered ogre rigs these games to renew paid debts and send innocent people into forced quarry labor.

At the table

Greet visitors by name, pull out a chair and explain every demand as though you are doing them a favor. Say, “Sit, friend. No need for a sour face over a number. One little game can put this behind us.” Keep smiling while you move the bench across the doorway; his hospitality is a means of control.

Personality

Initially friendly, sociable and reassuring. Deliberately sinister beneath that manner, he treats coerced labor as a business asset and knows that his supposed settlements are fraudulent.

Motivations

Keep paid debts alive through rigged games, profit from quarry labor contracts and preserve the appearance that his victims freely accepted the terms.

Adventure hook

A carter's sibling asks the party to stop a dawn wagon carrying three adult debtors whom Vorrak has trapped through rigged settlement games. His marked dice and the carters' original paid bonds expose the fraud. The party can demand a public reckoning before neutral witnesses, bring the evidence to the watch or free the captives from the counting-room storeroom. Vorrak offers their release for a safe exit if cornered, but blocks the door and fights if the party tries to take the captives or dice without a deal.
