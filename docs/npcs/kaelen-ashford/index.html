Kaelen Ashford

Human · Knight · Honour beyond the crown

Description

Resolute and protective, Kaelen wants to redeem himself without serving another corrupt lord. Battered plate armour, a burgundy cloak and a broken heraldic badge mark a knight who has lost his station but refuses to abandon his principles.

At the table

Stand between others and danger. Speak plainly, avoid claiming authority, and hesitate when asked whom you once served.

Adventure hook

He must rescue the heir whose disappearance he was blamed for—and needs allies willing to defy the crown.
