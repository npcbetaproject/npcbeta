Rurik Stoneward

Dwarf · Expedition leader · The road back to the mountain

Description

Steadfast and stubborn, Rurik is determined to reclaim his clan’s lost home. Weathered mail, a braided black beard and an ancestral axe accompany a leader who carries both the expedition’s hopes and the weight of its losses.

At the table

Check supplies before making promises. Remember each companion’s strengths, and resist suggestions that the lost hold should stay buried.

Adventure hook

Something inside the mountain is answering his signal fires. He believes survivors remain; his companions fear a trap.
