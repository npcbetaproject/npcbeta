Session

Tessa Brinewick

Gnome · Sailmaker · A stitch before the storm

Description

Tessa repairs sails in a cramped loft above the docks, working with quick hands and a running commentary on everyone else’s knots. Brisk and generous with her skills, she refuses to let a hurried captain take unsafe canvas to sea.

At the table

Keep stitching while you speak, tug each finished seam twice, and address people by their trade until they earn a name. Tessa wants her apprentices paid and every crew home safely; she becomes very still when someone dismisses a dangerous fault as good enough.

Adventure hook

Tessa finds neat knife cuts concealed beneath a fresh patch in a departing ship’s storm sail. She asks the party to warn the captain and examine the other sails before the evening tide; the damaged canvas bears the chalk mark of an apprentice who vanished after yesterday’s delivery.
