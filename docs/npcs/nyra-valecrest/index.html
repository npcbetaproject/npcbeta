Nyra Valecrest

Tiefling · Monster hunter · The truth behind the quarry

Description

Resourceful and guarded, Nyra hunts creatures others dismiss as folklore. Charcoal leather armour, swept-back horns and a scar across one eyebrow frame a hunter willing to question whether the supposed monster is the real threat.

At the table

Study tracks and exits while talking. Ask for evidence before accepting a story, and show unexpected patience toward frightened strangers.

Adventure hook

She discovers that her latest quarry protects a village—and that her employer wants the village destroyed.
