Session

Oswald Venn

Human · Deputy reeve · Every delay has its price

Description

Oswald oversees trading permits, market fines and the paperwork needed to move goods through town. Courteous and meticulously dressed, he invents small procedural obstacles, then hints that a private “expediting fee” will make them disappear.

At the table

Straighten a stack of papers, lower your voice for the price, and never call a bribe a bribe. Oswald wants a comfortable retirement and the protection of powerful traders; a witness or a request for a written receipt makes him suddenly careful.

Adventure hook

Oswald offers to waive the party’s permit fees if they recover a stolen ledger before an inspector arrives. He calls it routine municipal property, but its duplicate entries identify the merchants he has extorted—and payments for letting stolen goods pass the gate.
