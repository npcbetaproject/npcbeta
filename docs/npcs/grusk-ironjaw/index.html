Grusk Ironjaw

Half-orc · Extortionist and street enforcer · Every safe passage has a price

Description

Broad-shouldered and scarred, Grusk collects illegal “street dues” from merchants at the market and travelers entering town. His ragtag crew blocks wagons, steals small valuables and threatens anyone who refuses to pay.

At the table

Step close, crack your knuckles and speak in a low, falsely friendly voice. Grusk wants steady income and a fearsome reputation, not unnecessary bloodshed; he loses nerve if his crew deserts him or a witness mentions the town watch.

Adventure hook

A caravan merchant asks the party to stop Grusk's protection racket after several wagons are robbed. Grusk insists a corrupt watch officer takes a cut of the dues—and his collection ledger may prove it.
