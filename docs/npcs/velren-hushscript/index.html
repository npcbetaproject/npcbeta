Session

Velren Hushscript

Elf · Penitential scribe · Confessions that whisper demands

Description

Velren copies penitential accounts for adults staying in temple sanctuary and regards questions about his work with open suspicion. He steals sealed originals and animates scraps of their words into whispering threats, demanding payment to keep the accounts private.

At the table

Cover the folio with one hand, ask who authorized the visit and answer interruptions with clipped contempt. Say, “These are private papers. State your business, and keep your hands where I can see them.” His initial hostility conceals his own violation of the privacy he invokes.

Personality

Initially hostile, defensive and disdainful of scrutiny; he treats the trust of vulnerable guests as a resource to exploit and insists his copying fees entitle him to more than he was promised.

Motivations

Collect secret payments from sanctuary guests and retain their originals as leverage. He wants a profitable private archive beyond the temple’s oversight.

Adventure hook

A sanctuary guest asks the party to stop a folded paper slip that whispers her name and a demand for money outside her door. GM secret: Velren keeps the stolen originals in a red-thread-bound folio; while it remains tied, he can send the animated scraps after their authors. The party can seek willing testimony, trace the slips to his writing alcove or ask a steward to secure the folio. Untying its thread silences the scraps without exposing the sealed originals. Returning each account privately to its author and preserving the payment demands as evidence can end the extortion without turning the victims’ disclosures into public spectacle.
