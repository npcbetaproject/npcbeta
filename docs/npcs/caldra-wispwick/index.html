Session

Caldra Wispwick

Elf · Perfumer · The last scent of the grove

Description

Caldra blends perfumes in a tidy workroom, welcoming customers with patient questions and carefully remembered preferences. Her attentive hospitality conceals plans to destroy the wild moonviolet grove and make her stored stock the only local supply.

At the table

Offer a clean testing cloth, listen without interrupting and recall one detail from a visitor's last purchase. Say, “You need not choose in a hurry. Tell me the scent you hoped to find, and I will help.” Keep her voice warm when challenged; she describes another person's hardship as an unfortunate cost of doing business.

Personality

Initially friendly, gracious and observant. Deliberately sinister beneath that manner, she is willing to destroy a shared resource and deny healers supplies to gain control of the trade.

Motivations

Eliminate the wild source of moonviolets, force perfumers and healers to buy her reserves at inflated prices and keep her polished reputation intact.

Adventure hook

Caldra's apprentice learns that hired burners will destroy the forest's only moonviolet grove at midnight and brings the party a route sketch and Caldra's signed payment order. The flowers also supply local healers, so the party can escort the apprentice to the watch and stop the crew at the forest clearing, or confront Caldra with the evidence and demand a witnessed cancellation. She offers a share of her stored flowers for silence; saving the grove preserves a source she cannot control.
