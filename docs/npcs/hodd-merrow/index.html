Session

Hodd Merrow

Human · Collier · Collars beneath the charcoal

Description

Soot-stained and coughing, Hodd has tended woodland charcoal kilns for twenty years. Since gnolls seized his wife Tamsin, the quiet collier has hidden iron collars in his deliveries to a cult, knowing what they are for.

At the table

Speak softly between coughs, mutter to the kiln, and agree with anyone who blames him: “Aye. You’re right.” Use Commoner statistics, armed with a shovel; Survival +4. He can guide the party to the white stone and recognize the handler’s face.

Personality

Withdrawn and distracted at first, then painfully compliant; guilt makes him accept accusations without defending himself.

Motivations

Recover Tamsin alive. He fears she is already dead and that every collar he delivers condemns someone else.

Adventure hook

Hodd offers to lead the party to the white stone if they help recover Tamsin from the Rot-Laugh Pack, a Yeenoghu war band holding captives for Bloodwin. Skarl Half-Ear, the scarred Pack Lord, promises a feast to keep his hungry gnolls obedient; Vhrekka, a Fang of Yeenoghu, may break the ban on eating prisoners. Her half-starved hyenas obey only her. The Laughers flank vulnerable targets, retreat and return at night, tracking by scent; they scatter if their leaders fall. Killing the handler, spilling blood near the cages or turning Vhrekka against Skarl may fracture the pack.
