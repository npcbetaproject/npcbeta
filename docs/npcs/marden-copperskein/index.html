Session

Marden Copperskein

Human · Bell founder · Bronze before the river rises

Description

Marden casts bells for chapels, farms and riverside settlements, greeting every visitor as a welcome extra pair of hands. He talks through each task with cheerful patience and offers a seat even while his foundry is busy.

At the table

Greet strangers by name as soon as you hear it, offer water, and demonstrate a point by tapping a little bronze test bell. Say, “Come in, friend. Have you a good ear, a strong back, or news from the river?” His warmth becomes focused urgency when the warning bell is mentioned.

Personality

Initially friendly, talkative and encouraging; quick to trust a practical offer of help and careful never to promise work he cannot finish.

Motivations

Restore the flood warning before heavy rain reaches the valley, pay his crew and recover the bronze bought with the village's pooled savings.

Adventure hook

The village's cracked flood-warning bell must be recast before the forecast rains, but Marden opens the paid-for bronze shipment and finds crates packed with stone. He asks the party to follow the carrier's tally marks to a warehouse by the ferry and recover the metal while his crew prepares the mould; a matching seal on a crate awaiting an upstream barge gives them a lead before morning departure.
