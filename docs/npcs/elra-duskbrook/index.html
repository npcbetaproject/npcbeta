Session

Elra Duskbrook

Human · Desperate laborer · A silver pendant for a few coins

Description

Exhausted and nervous, Elra offers a silver pendant for far less than its value. She stole it to pay for her brother’s medicine.

At the table

Twist your apron, speak quickly when naming the price, and avoid looking at the pendant. Elra needs medicine for her brother today; she will admit the theft if someone offers a way to help without abandoning him.

Adventure hook

The pendant bears a local noble’s crest, and its owner is searching for it.
