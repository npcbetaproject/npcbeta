Session

Morla Fennstitch

Halfling · Saddler · A sound saddle needs a rested mule

Description

Morla fits pack saddles for departing caravans, answering questions without looking up from her stitching. This practical halfling charges fairly and refuses work that will put an exhausted animal back on the road.

At the table

Keep checking a strap while speaking, ask for measurements before explanations and give precise estimates. Say, “Delivery at dawn? Find a rested mule first. I can fit the harness while you settle the loan.” Her initial indifference comes from a crowded workbench, not contempt for the customer.

Personality

Initially indifferent, absorbed in her work and blunt about practical limits; meticulous, fair-minded and difficult to rush into a bad decision.

Motivations

Keep her small workshop solvent, send travelers out with reliable equipment and make sure the animals carrying it can manage the journey.

Adventure hook

An urgent mountain dispatch must leave at dawn, but its courier's mule is exhausted. Morla will adapt a spare harness at cost if the party arranges a replacement. A caravan master at the stables will lend a rested mule in exchange for collecting two escaped pack goats from a nearby paddock; the courier also needs persuading to leave his familiar mule behind. With both agreements secured, Morla can finish the fitting before departure.
