Session

Nivra Copperchime

Gnome · Clock repairer · The alarm is part of the price

Description

Nivra repairs the town's warning clock with a flat voice and an exact account of every part. He has accepted payment to leave its alarm silent while raiders empty the granary.

At the table

Count small parts, answer only the question asked and glance toward the mechanism when someone mentions the time. Say, “It will be ready when I said. Do you need a repair, or are you here to watch me work?” His initial indifference remains matter-of-fact when he starts bargaining over his secret.

Personality

Initially indifferent, methodical and strictly transactional; meticulous about mechanisms, but willing to endanger others when the payment suits him.

Motivations

Collect the raiders' promised share, make the clock failure appear accidental and retain the repair contract that gives him trusted access to the alarm.

Adventure hook

A granary porter finds a raider's payment note naming the hour when the warning clock will be silent. Nivra has fitted a sham alarm linkage beneath the gatehouse mechanism, and the raid is due tonight. The party can inspect and restore the linkage, arrange an independent alarm or warn the granary watch while preserving the note as evidence. Nivra offers the raiders' meeting place in exchange for safe custody if exposed; left unwatched, he tries to remove the faulty parts before they can be examined.
