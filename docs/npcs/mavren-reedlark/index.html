Session

Mavren Reedlark

Human · Ferryman · No wages, no crossing

Description

Mavren runs a small river ferry and greets unfamiliar passengers with a suspicious scowl. He distrusts easy promises, but keeps his own bargains and pays his crew before taking a share.

At the table

Block the gangplank with a boot, inspect an offered coin and demand a straight answer. Say, “Keep back from the rope. Have you come to help, or just tell me how to run my boat?” His initial hostility softens after useful work, though he never becomes chatty.

Personality

Initially hostile, defensive about his competence and quick to suspect a trick; stubbornly fair to workers and reliable once terms are settled.

Motivations

Recover the crew's wages, keep the ferry operating and prove that one mishap has not made him unfit to run the crossing.

Adventure hook

A loaded punt breaks loose during a squall, leaving Mavren's locked toll box wedged beneath it on a shingle bank upstream. The box holds his crew's overdue wages, and they will not launch the ferry until they are paid. He offers the party free passage and the loan of a small skiff to recover it before rising water covers the bank. They can row to the punt or reach it on foot through the old ford, but must shift it without letting the box wash away.
