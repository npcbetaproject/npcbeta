Session

Dagna Slatewake

Dwarf · Miller · The wheel has stopped, but the rent has not

Description

Dagna runs a riverside grain mill with flour on her sleeves and an iron-gray braid over one shoulder. She challenges unfamiliar visitors from the doorway, convinced that another polite inquiry means someone wants her work for less.

At the table

Plant your feet in the doorway and brush flour from a sleeve while listening. Start sharply: “If you've come to tell me water runs downhill, save your breath. Someone upstream has stopped it.” Let specific offers of work earn a grudging invitation inside.

Personality

Initially hostile, suspicious and curt; she respects people who keep a bargain and refuses to pass her losses on to hungry customers.

Motivations

Get the mill turning before her customers' damp grain spoils and keep her two workers paid.

Adventure hook

A new timber dam upstream has diverted the millrace into an estate's private fishpond. Dagna asks the party to accompany her to the river ford, document the diversion and open the old sluice; hired guards hold the bank, and pulling the wrong timbers could flood the nearby farm.
