Harthos Ravenmoor

Orc · Ferryman · Patient keeper of the crossing

Description

Patient and practical, Harthos carries people across the river with steady hands and little ceremony. Weathered clothing and a wooden ferry pole reflect years spent reading currents and making room for one more traveler.

At the table

Allow comfortable silences. Check the river while talking, explain hazards matter-of-factly, and never rush a frightened passenger.

Adventure hook

The ferry keeps drifting toward a submerged stone arch against the current. Harthos wants someone to learn what lies below.
