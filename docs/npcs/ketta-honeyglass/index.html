Ketta Honeyglass

Goblin · Beekeeper · The hive has its own counsel

Description

Ketta tends a hillside apiary with patient hands and a careful ear for the moods of her bees. She is practical and softly amused, though she cannot explain why her workers return each dawn carrying pollen from a flower that grows nowhere nearby.

At the table

Pause to listen before answering. Touch the edge of a hive gently, name each queen's mood as if giving a weather report, and offer visitors a little honey before discussing payment.

Adventure hook

Ketta's hives have gone silent after moonrise, and each morning her bees leave a small wax key at the door. She asks the party to follow the workers to a shuttered apiary before the key softens in the sun.
