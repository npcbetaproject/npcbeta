Session

Brammok Tilehorn

Minotaur · Stone mason · A friendly face across a closed tunnel

Description

Brammok repairs mountain stonework and welcomes travelers with cheerful advice. He plants his broad frame across a damaged tunnel entrance until the missing support crew is found and the passage can be made safe.

At the table

Offer a seat on a stone block, praise useful questions and sketch the problem in dust with one thick finger. Say, “Good to see willing hands! Tea first, then we find my crew. Nobody goes under that lintel until it can hold its own weight.”

Personality

Initially friendly, patient and eager to explain his craft; stubborn about safety and deeply loyal to the people working beside him.

Motivations

Bring the crew home, keep the mountain route useful and prove that closing a dangerous passage is an act of care rather than a demand for tribute.

Adventure hook

Two adult workers sent to inspect a side opening have not returned, and fresh tool taps sound beyond a fall of loose stone. Brammok offers his repair fee to companions who reach the opening by the mountain trail and locate the crew. The party can help plan a rescue and a temporary detour, or convince him that an alternative support plan is sound. Travelers who try to force the entrance or dismantle the warning barrier meet a dangerous opponent: he grapples intruders and drives them back, but stops when they withdraw or agree to a safe inspection.
