Session

Varruk “Copperhand” Drenn

Half-orc · Thug and protection racketeer · A copper for your peace of mind

Description

Varruk collects “stall insurance” from town merchants, pocketing their copper with a courteous smile and a quiet threat. His crew of three petty crooks blocks carts, harasses travelers near the gates and breaks merchandise when someone refuses to pay.

At the table

Speak softly, straighten a copper ring before naming a price, and call every threat a friendly warning. Varruk wants a steady income without attracting the watch; he bluffs about powerful backers and loses confidence when victims stand together. His crew includes a lookout, a cutpurse and a cudgel carrier, loyal only while the money lasts.

Adventure hook

A merchant hires the party to escort her cart through town without paying Varruk. When his crew blocks the lane, the youngest lookout slips the party a collection ledger: it names every victim, and Varruk intends to burn a refusing merchant’s stall after sunset.
