Session

Calder Venn

Human · Infantry signaler · A call from the old road

Description

A mud-streaked infantry signaler wanders into the village at dawn, still in patched armor and carrying a cracked brass whistle. Calder asks whether anyone has seen his patrol and keeps glancing toward the rooftops, waiting for a signal no one else recognizes.

At the table

Tap the brass whistle with a thumb, count doors and windows before sitting, and give clipped orders before catching yourself and apologizing. Calder is disciplined and protective of civilians; he wants to find his missing squad but fears his retreat signal left them behind.

Personality

Courteous, alert and methodical, Calder hides his guilt behind military routine.

Motivations

He is searching for his missing patrol and privately believes he sounded the retreat too early.

Adventure hook

At dusk, Calder repeats his squad’s two-note call from the abandoned mill. A reply answers from the north road, and fresh bootprints lead from the road’s bend to a buried military marker. He asks the party to follow the trail before whoever answered reaches the village.
