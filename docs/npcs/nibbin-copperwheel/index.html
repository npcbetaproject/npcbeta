Nibbin Copperwheel

Goblin · Goblin traveling merchant · Every bargain has a story

Description

Cheerful and shrewd, Nibbin sells practical supplies and peculiar bargains from a cluttered wagon. He remembers every customer—and what they bought.

At the table

Rub your palms together, greet returning customers by their last purchase, and offer a practical bargain before an oddity. Nibbin wants repeat customers, but his careful inventory cannot explain the knocking beneath his wagon.

Adventure hook

Something hidden beneath his wagon starts knocking. He insists it wasn’t there yesterday.
