Session

Nollis Barrelbrook

Gnome · Traveling cooper · A sound hoop before the water runs out

Description

Nollis repairs barrels for caravans along a dry trade road and greets each arriving wagon like an old friend. A split hoop has left a convoy’s drinking-water cask leaking at his roadside workbench.

At the table

Welcome visitors, offer a place in the shade and enthusiastically explain one useful detail about a barrel. Say, “Come into the shade! Hold this end, and I will show you how we keep everyone’s water inside the wood.” He is initially friendly, helpful and talkative.

Personality

Initially friendly and eager to include strangers in useful work; proud of careful repairs, generous with advice and prone to explaining a craft problem long after others understand it.

Motivations

Keep the convoy supplied until its next watering place, earn repeat work through reliable repairs and teach someone a skill they can use after he moves on.

Adventure hook

Nollis asks the party to borrow a sound iron hoop from a carter resting at the nearby caravanserai. The carter needs that hoop for an empty cask promised to another customer; the party can arrange a replacement, negotiate a short loan or help transfer the water into clean spare vessels while Nollis repairs the damaged hoop. He supplies his labor and will accept a delayed departure rather than risk the convoy’s drinking water. Saving the supply earns a dependable traveling craftsman and introductions along the route.
