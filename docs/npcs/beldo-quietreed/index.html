Session

Beldo Quietreed

Halfling · Shrine organist · Four notes before the doors open

Description

Beldo prepares music for a hillside shrine and answers interruptions without looking up from his score. While restoring its disused organ to service, he recognizes four unwanted notes as an old warning from the shrine’s enchanted waterworks.

At the table

Keep a finger on the score, count a beat before answering and ask visitors to wait until the phrase ends. Say, “If you need a procession tune, leave the order there. If you can hear those four notes, stop the door steward.” His indifference becomes focused urgency when someone can help.

Personality

Initially indifferent, distracted by rehearsal and sparing with conversation; methodical, dependable and willing to postpone a performance rather than put listeners at risk.

Motivations

Reopen the organ for public use, preserve the previous organist’s practical annotations and keep the shrine’s guests safe without turning a solvable problem into panic.

Adventure hook

An old score labels the repeated four-note phrase “cistern overflow”; the organ’s enchantment was designed to carry warnings through the shrine. A crowded service begins within the hour, but nobody has inspected the cistern chamber since the organ fell silent. Beldo asks the party to alert the stewards and help keep visitors outside while the caretaker checks the waterworks. They can locate the caretaker’s missing inspection ledger, arrange a service in the courtyard or find safe access for an inspection. Beldo offers music and introductions for a future gathering in return, and accepts moving the service rather than rushing a repair.
