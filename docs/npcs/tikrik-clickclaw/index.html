Tikrik Clickclaw

Kobold · Trap maker · Mind the pressure plate

Description

A meticulous kobold carrying wire, wooden wedges and mismatched tools, Tikrik mutters about careless workmanship while resetting dungeon pressure plates. He resents adventurers who break his traps but admires anyone clever enough to disarm them.

At the table

Click your tongue at damaged mechanisms and explain repairs with fussy precision. Tikrik wants his work respected; he pauses his repairs to warn anyone who shows an honest interest in his craft.

Adventure hook

Someone keeps sabotaging Tikrik’s repairs. He offers a safe route through the dungeon if the party catches the culprit—and leaves his favourite trap intact.
