Session

Elaris Oakrhyme

Elf · Itinerant poet · A final verse between two tables

Description

Elaris trades songs for supper in mountain-road taverns, eagerly inviting strangers to join the chorus. Two retired drovers remember different endings to a song that may describe a lost travelers’ cache, and she wants both accounts heard before anyone sets out.

At the table

Pull out a chair, offer to share the bread and ask visitors which songs they grew up with. Say, “Sit here! I have three verses, two splendidly stubborn witnesses and room for another opinion.” Let her listen enthusiastically rather than perform over someone else’s answer.

Personality

Initially friendly, welcoming and delightfully talkative; curious about ordinary people’s memories and careful to give each singer credit.

Motivations

Preserve the song with its singers’ names, discover what the missing verse actually describes and keep the search from becoming a contest over who deserves to be remembered.

Adventure hook

Elaris gathers the party at a taproom’s long table with two adult drovers: one recalls a stone arch, the other a fallen bell. Each refuses to sing beside the other after an old quarrel over an unpaid room. Elaris offers introductions along the mountain road if the party can hear both versions, settle the room debt or arrange separate performances, then compare the landmarks before searching. The cache contains travelers’ emergency provisions; Elaris argues for recording and replenishing it rather than carrying everything away.
