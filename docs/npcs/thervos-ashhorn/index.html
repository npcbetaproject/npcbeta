Session

Thervos Ashhorn

Minotaur · Estate fencing master · A welcome at a closed door

Description

Thervos teaches fencing to a noble household and greets visitors with cheerful encouragement, even while barring its practice hall. Two missing training blades and an adult guest’s reckless challenge have convinced him that the doors must stay closed until everyone agrees to safe terms.

At the table

Offer a towel, praise good footwork and make room to talk beside the doorway without letting anyone pass. Say, “Welcome! I’ll gladly hear your challenge after we find my practice blades and agree nobody fights angry.” His warmth is sincere; the closed door is a boundary, not a taunt.

Personality

Initially friendly, helpful and enthusiastic about teaching; calm under insult, stubborn about consent and protective of inexperienced students.

Motivations

Recover the household’s training blades, prevent an angry guest from turning a lesson into a dangerous duel and show that discipline matters more than noble rank.

Adventure hook

A guest asks the party to get past Thervos and open the practice hall before a public challenge. Thervos offers a lesson and introductions if they instead recover the missing blades and persuade the challenger to withdraw the insult or accept a supervised demonstration. A servant saw the blades carried toward the armory for polishing but cannot leave a post to check. The party can trace the loan, negotiate an apology or arrange another day. If anyone forces entry or threatens a student, Thervos blocks and disarms them, using restraint rather than pursuing a defeated opponent. Returning the blades and agreeing to safe terms ends the standoff.
