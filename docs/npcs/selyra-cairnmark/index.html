Session

Selyra Cairnmark

Elf · Boundary surveyor · Two deeds and one well

Description

Selyra surveys field boundaries with a plumb bob, a measuring cord and little patience for spectators. Her ochre coat is dusty at the hem, and she treats anyone touching a marker as a likely source of another day's work.

At the table

Hold a cord taut and order visitors off the line before acknowledging them. Say, “Move your boot. Unless you intend to argue with the measurement as well.” Challenge vague claims, but listen closely when someone supplies a date, landmark or original deed.

Personality

Initially hostile, blunt and defensive of her work; she applies the same measurements to powerful landowners and small tenants.

Motivations

Establish a boundary people can verify and preserve lawful access to the village's only reliable well.

Adventure hook

Two apparently valid deeds place the communal well on opposite sides of an estate boundary. Selyra asks the party to escort her to an abandoned logging camp and uncover the oldest carved survey stone beneath a collapsed timber stack; the estate steward arrives tomorrow to fence the access path, and both claimants insist on sending witnesses.
