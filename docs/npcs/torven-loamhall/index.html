Session

Torven Loamhall

Human · Messenger · Two sealed chances to make peace

Description

Torven carries private letters between towns, answering questions with a suspicious glare and a demand for clear instructions. He resents being mistaken for a spy and guards his customers' seals as carefully as his own pay.

At the table

Keep one hand on the satchel flap, ask who sent the speaker and cut off guesses about a letter's contents. Say, “You can doubt my manners. You don't get to doubt my seals. Are you helping me reach that door or wasting my daylight?” His initial hostility is defensive pride, not a wish to start a fight.

Personality

Initially hostile, prickly and quick to challenge assumptions; scrupulous about privacy and stubbornly committed to finishing an accepted delivery.

Motivations

Earn the acknowledgments that complete his contract, protect the trust attached to his courier's mark and prove that plain dealing can survive a family feud.

Adventure hook

Torven holds reconciliation letters for the heads of two feuding households, but both gates are closed to unfamiliar visitors. At dawn the families intend to divide their shared bridge and end all cooperation. He offers the party a fee to secure a neutral guarantor and persuade each household to accept its sealed letter. They can negotiate separate deliveries or arrange a witnessed exchange in a public courtyard; opening the letters would undermine the trust needed to make either plan work.
