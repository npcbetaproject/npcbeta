Session

Tovin Mossribbon

Halfling · Temple floor polisher · A stone pilgrim across the procession route

Description

Tovin polishes a temple’s worn floors and cheerfully guides visitors around the wet patches. An ancient stone pilgrim has fallen asleep across the usual procession route, leaving the stewards divided over whether to wake it or change their plans.

At the table

Offer visitors a dry cloth to wipe their boots, introduce everyone by name and explain the problem as though inviting help with a shared chore. Say, “Mind the shine, friend. Our guest has chosen a grand place for a nap—shall we find everyone a little room?” She is initially friendly and eager to hear a workable suggestion.

Personality

Initially friendly, talkative and patient with confused visitors; she respects the stone pilgrim as a guest and dislikes treating ceremony as more important than people.

Motivations

Keep the floor and visitors safe, let the procession proceed with dignity and help the old pilgrim reach the resting place it was seeking.

Adventure hook

Tovin asks the party to speak with the stewards before the procession assembles. The pilgrim’s wrist inscription names the west cloister as its destination, but that door is locked and the caretaker has not heard about its arrival. The party can find the caretaker, arrange a quieter route through the courtyard or wait for the pilgrim to wake and offer directions. Tovin supplies cloths to mark a dry path and accepts postponing the ceremony if no suitable route is available. Helping both visitors and pilgrim earns a welcoming temple contact.
