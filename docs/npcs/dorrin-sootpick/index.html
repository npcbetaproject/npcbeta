Session

Dorrin Sootpick

Dwarf · Artificer & trap disarmer · Listen before you touch

Description

Dorrin crawls deeper than most treasure hunters dare, disarming dungeon traps with patient hands and ingenious little tools. Mud cakes his knees, stone dust clings to his beard, and his scraped goggles rarely stay clean for a minute.

At the table

Stop mid-sentence to listen for a hidden mechanism, sort salvaged springs by touch, and insist everyone stand behind the chalk line. Dorrin treats a well-made trap with professional respect and gets cross when someone calls careful work luck.

Personality

Methodical, dryly funny and quietly protective, with more patience for mechanisms than boasting adventurers.

Motivations

He wants to bring every hired companion back alive and recover the ingenious mechanisms other delvers smash for scrap.

Adventure hook

Dorrin has wedged open a pressure plate three levels below a burial vault, but someone is resetting the traps behind him. His chalk marks now lead toward an unexplored stair; he needs companions to guard his back while he finds whoever is changing the route.
