Session

Gravven Hollowmoss

Dwarf · Shrine quarry agent · An omen priced by the cartload

Description

Gravven arranges stone deliveries and repairs for a remote shrine with brisk, transactional efficiency. He secretly sells its consecrated foundation stones to collectors and circulates a fabricated omen so the caretakers will abandon the site before an inventory exposes him.

At the table

Check the delivery tally, ask which invoice a visitor means and quote transport costs before discussing the shrine. Say, “Stone, distance and number of carts. Prophecies are the caretakers’ business.” He treats the invented omen as someone else’s problem.

Personality

Initially indifferent, professional and impatient with unpriced requests; calculating about other people’s beliefs and willing to endanger a cherished building to conceal his theft.

Motivations

Keep the proceeds from his illicit sales and secure the last collector’s payment before the shrine’s inventory. His authority over deliveries and a hired carter conceal the removals, but the collector’s receipts identify the stones and contradict his account.

Adventure hook

A shrine caretaker asks the party why empty repair crates leave heavier than they arrive. GM secret: Gravven has sold stones marked in the shrine’s old dedication register and spread a false prediction that the site must be deserted before the next moonrise. A buyer is due at the roadside campsite tomorrow. The party can compare the register with the buyer’s receipt, persuade the carter to hold the shipment or bring the caretakers evidence before they leave. Gravven offers the buyer’s identity in exchange for concealing his part. Recovering the stones and arranging an independent safety inspection protects the shrine; exposing the lie lets the caretakers decide its future without coercion.
