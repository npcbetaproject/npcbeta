Session

Orsa Cinderglass

Dwarf · Glazier · Wax where iron should be

Description

Orsa cuts and leads stained glass for chapels and prosperous households, checking every measurement twice. Busy with a delivery, she gives visitors the same level stare she gives a pane that does not quite fit.

At the table

Keep measuring while visitors speak, then ask for the detail that affects the job. Say, “Hold that thought—and this edge. Which window, exactly?” A precise answer gets her full attention; flattery does not.

Personality

Initially indifferent, absorbed and methodical; dryly humorous once the work is safe, she values careful hands over impressive claims.

Motivations

Keep her customers safe, protect her apprentices' reputations and find who tampered with her finished work.

Adventure hook

During a final inspection, Orsa finds that two iron support pins beneath a heavy chapel window have been replaced with dark wax. She asks the party to keep worshippers out of that aisle and fetch the caretaker's key to the locked scaffold gate while she braces the frame; the evening service starts within the hour, and fresh wax scraps on the scaffold may identify the saboteur.
