Riggles Flintnose

Deep gnome · Gem prospector and trader · A nose for buried trouble

Description

Cautious and soft-spoken, Riggles hunts gems in the deep tunnels and trades his finds in small, carefully weighed lots. His unusually acute nose distinguishes ore dust, lamp oils and familiar belongings, making him a useful finder of lost items when a trail is fresh.

At the table

Sniff twice before answering, keep your back near a wall, and ask who else knows about the job. Riggles wants enough trade to support his mining crew without revealing their richest seam; strong perfume and smoke can overwhelm his nose.

Adventure hook

Riggles recognizes his crew’s distinctive violet ore dust on a merchant’s boots, though their latest shipment never reached the surface. He offers a share of the recovered gems if the party escorts him through the old workings beneath the market; the missing miners may still be alive.
