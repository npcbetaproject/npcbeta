Wenrin Duskshield

Human · Guard captain · Weary keeper of the gate

Description

Dutiful, watchful and weary, Wenrin keeps the town guard disciplined while quietly shouldering every missed patrol and sleepless night. Practical armor and a burgundy cloak mark a captain who values service above ceremony.

At the table

Scan the room before speaking. Keep instructions short, rub a tired brow, and show relief when someone follows through.

Adventure hook

A patrol has failed to return from an abandoned watchtower. Wenrin needs discreet help before panic spreads.
