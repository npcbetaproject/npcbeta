Captain Mara Thornwick

Human · Bandit captain · Safe passage has a price

Description

Charming in town and threatening on the road, Mara buys strangers drinks while her crew assesses their purses and travel plans. She prefers easy targets and abandons a fight when the cost outweighs the gain.

At the table

Offer a friendly bargain, ask casually when travelers leave, and keep an escape route in sight. Mara wants steady income from intimidation, but a reckless crew member is undermining her promises.

Adventure hook

Mara offers safe passage for a fee, but someone in her crew robs travelers who have already paid. She proposes a temporary truce if the party helps expose the culprit.
