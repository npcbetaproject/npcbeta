Session

Nella Ruskettle

Halfling · Rat catcher · Something stronger than rat bait

Description

Nella clears vermin from the town's storehouses with wooden live traps and an exact account of every catch. She meets unfamiliar customers with narrowed eyes, convinced that most people conceal the worst part of a job until the price is settled.

At the table

Keep one hand on a closed trap and demand the whole story before naming a price. Say, “If it were only rats, you would have said where.” Let practical evidence earn her attention, though reassurance alone does not soften her.

Personality

Initially hostile, suspicious and blunt; she takes pride in careful work and refuses to endanger children or working animals with careless poison.

Motivations

Find who is dosing grain near her traps, protect her livelihood and keep contaminated sacks out of the town's food supply.

Adventure hook

Nella finds grain soaked in sleeping draught beside the granary's rear door, along with a dropped apothecary bottle. She asks the party to trace the bottle and watch the door while she separates the suspect sacks; the morning bread delivery leaves at dawn, and she suspects someone intends to drug the granary dogs before stealing from the stores.
