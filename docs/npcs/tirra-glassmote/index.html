Session

Tirra Glassmote

Gnome · Dream interpreter · Kind words behind borrowed dreams

Description

Tirra welcomes anxious sleepers to a curtained consultation room, remembers their worries and offers polished glass discs to keep beneath their pillows. Her warm attention conceals a scheme to turn reassuring dreams into commands her clients obey.

At the table

Lean forward, remember one small concern and speak as though there is plenty of time. Say, “You have carried this worry alone long enough. Tell me what you saw, and we will make sense of it together.” Keep her welcome gentle even while she steers questions away from the underside of the glass discs.

Personality

Initially friendly, attentive and patient. Deliberately sinister beneath that manner, she treats private fears as tools for controlling people and feels entitled to the wealth she takes from them.

Motivations

Bind prosperous clients to dream-borne commands, make them appear to surrender valuables willingly in public and preserve her reputation as a trusted adviser.

Adventure hook

A client asks the party for help after dreaming the exact command to bring her savings to Tirra at midnight; the same spiral appears in the dream and beneath Tirra's gift disc. The party can pose as new clients to inspect the consultation room, where a brass bowl beneath a loose floorboard links the discs. Removing a client's disc prevents further commands to that sleeper; breaking the bowl's spiral frees every linked client before the collection; Tirra offers a share of the stolen wealth for silence if exposed.
