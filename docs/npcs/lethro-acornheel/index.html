Lethro Acornheel

Satyr · Boundary guide · A courteous pause at the thorn arch

Description

Lethro guides travelers along the woodland boundary, welcoming them with an easy smile and a choice of routes. This horned satyr explains the crossing's restrictions before asking for payment, and accepts useful news as readily as bread.

At the table

Offer two clear choices, tilt your head as though listening to the trees and thank anyone who asks before touching a marker. Say, “Welcome, friends. A little patience here will save us all a long walk.” Keep his voice kind when he refuses passage; friendliness does not make him abandon his duty.

Personality

Initially friendly, patient and curious about travelers; generous with local knowledge, but firm about agreements that protect the path and neighboring farms.

Motivations

Keep the boundary's living thorn barrier contained, earn a modest living guiding crossings and persuade hurried travelers that a safe route is worth a short delay.

Adventure hook

A wagon clips the boundary stone, dropping its capstone into the ravine below. Until it is repaired, uncovered iron makes the thorn arch close across the path. Lethro offers a longer detour or a quiet crossing with iron gear wrapped in felt if the party helps retrieve and replace the capstone. Repair earns his escort; trying to smash the stone or burn through the barrier makes him block the party by force, because breaking the boundary would release the thorns toward nearby farms.
