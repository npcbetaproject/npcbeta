Maera Cinderhand

Dwarf · Weaponsmith · Good steel needs a steady hand

Description

Maera forges dependable weapons and repairs battered gear at a soot-darkened village forge. She promises an honest edge rather than miracles: “Fire can forge you fine weapons. Keeping them sharp is your part.”

At the table

Inspect imaginary blade edges while speaking, then set the tool down when something matters. Plain-spoken and proud of her craft, Maera wants her work to protect people; she refuses rushed commissions that would leave a dangerous flaw.

Adventure hook

A traveller brings Maera a broken sword stamped with her maker’s mark. She recognizes a repair she made for a caravan guard missing since a robbery, and asks the party to trace the traveller’s story before the watch blames the missing guard for the theft.
