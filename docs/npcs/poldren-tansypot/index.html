Session

Poldren Tansypot

Halfling · Roadside cook · Supper without breaking a promise

Description

Poldren serves hot meals from a cooking wagon at a busy trade-road stopping place, answering questions while counting portions. A closed ford has stranded more drovers than he can feed, but he refuses to open a food consignment already promised to the next waystation.

At the table

Count bowls before faces, keep stirring during introductions and ask how many people need feeding. Say, “How many portions, and what can you trade? Those sacks belong to the next stop.” His indifference comes from concentrating on the work, not a lack of concern.

Personality

Initially indifferent, busy and strictly practical; dependable about other people’s supplies and quietly pleased when a useful plan feeds everyone without breaking a promise.

Motivations

Give the stranded adults a proper supper, deliver the promised consignment intact and keep his cooking wagon welcome along the trade road.

Adventure hook

A closed ford leaves adult drovers camped beside Poldren’s wagon with cold travel rations and little dry fuel. He asks the party to negotiate for spare firewood and cooking supplies at the nearby caravanserai, organize a shared meal from the travelers’ own provisions or arrange a sheltered cooking place until the crossing reopens. He will cook and share his remaining ingredients, but will not take from the sealed onward consignment. Successful helpers receive meals at his next roadside stop and introductions to drovers who know the route.
