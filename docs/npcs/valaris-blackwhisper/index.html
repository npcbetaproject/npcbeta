Valaris Blackwhisper

Human · Gravedigger · Discretion among the headstones

Description

Respectful and discreet, Valaris tends graves with the same care given to the living who visit them. A simple hood, work gloves and a shovel identify someone trusted with difficult tasks and stories best left unspoken.

At the table

Lower your voice near mourners. Ask permission before touching keepsakes, and answer questions carefully without turning grief into gossip.

Adventure hook

A newly filled grave is neatly reopened each dawn, yet no tracks disturb the surrounding earth. Valaris asks for a quiet investigation.
