Orrik Ashmuzzle

Werewolf · Camp lookout · A welcome behind the warning rope

Description

Orrik keeps watch over woodcutters sheltering in an abandoned logging camp, speaking cheerfully through a grey-furred wolf's muzzle. He welcomes travelers to the fire and explains the double-knotted ropes that mark firm ground through the surrounding bog.

At the table

Greet each arrival warmly, offer a dry seat and explain the rope markers with patient enthusiasm. Say, “Welcome, friend. Follow the double knots and you will miss the bog.” His voice drops when anyone cuts a safety rope or tries to force a loaded cart onto the untested shortcut; he offers one clear warning before blocking the route.

Personality

Initially friendly, helpful and eager to put strangers at ease; protective of the workers in his care and stubborn when a profitable shortcut puts them at risk.

Motivations

Keep the woodcutters safe, restore a reliable route before the next shift and earn respect for his work rather than fear of his shape.

Adventure hook

A timber buyer's crew removes Orrik's safety ropes, insisting that a shorter bog route will carry more carts. Workers return before dawn, and Orrik's sounding pole sinks through the new path's crust. He offers the party free guidance if they persuade the foreman to restore the marked route and help check the crossing. If anyone deliberately cuts the remaining ropes or forces loaded carts onto the unsafe ground, he will physically block them.
