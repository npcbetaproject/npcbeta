Session

Dellen Quincebrook

Halfling · Taproom skittles keeper · A kingpin missing before the match

Description

Dellen maintains a taproom’s skittles lane and welcomes newcomers with patient demonstrations. The weighted kingpin has vanished before a visiting team’s match, and the regulars are starting to blame their guests.

At the table

Offer to show visitors the lane, remember their names and make room for beginners. Say, “Come in, friends. Help me find the old kingpin, and I’ll teach you the house throw.” He is initially friendly and wants both teams to enjoy the evening.

Personality

Initially friendly, talkative and generous with encouragement; he values fair play more than his regulars’ pride and dislikes accusations made without evidence.

Motivations

Recover the kingpin, keep the match fair and make visiting players feel welcome rather than suspected.

Adventure hook

Dellen asks the party to check the lane and adjoining storeroom before the teams gather. The kingpin’s brass-weighted base has left a circular mark on a recently moved drinks tray, and a server remembers clearing spare game pieces with the empty cups. The party can follow the tray’s route, speak with the staff or help Dellen organize a friendly practice round while he searches. The pin turns out to be packed with returned festival decorations, not stolen by a rival. Finding it and clearing the visitors’ names earns free use of the lane and a sociable taproom contact.
