Gribbin Brassnose

Goblin · Treasure seeker · First pick of the dusty riches

Description

An eager goblin scavenger with an oversized satchel and a bent crowbar, Gribbin taps stones for hollow spaces and insists every dusty trinket is valuable. He offers to split any treasure—after he gets first pick.

At the table

Peer into every crack, pocket harmless scraps, and whisper loudly about hidden riches. Gribbin wants a valuable find and a capable companion, but his enthusiasm outruns his caution.

Adventure hook

Gribbin found a locked coffer behind a burial niche. Something inside whispers directions to more treasure, and he wants help following them.
