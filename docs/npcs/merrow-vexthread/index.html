Session

Merrow Vexthread

Halfling · Tailor · Every private word has a price

Description

Merrow fits formal coats with exacting skill and a sneer for anyone questioning his prices. Hidden listening charms let him overhear negotiating envoys, whose private concessions become the terms of his blackmail.

At the table

Brush an imaginary speck from a sleeve, criticize a customer's posture and refuse casual handling of the cloth. Say, “Keep your hands off that coat. If you want explanations, pay for a fitting.” His initial hostility is brittle pride; he becomes calculating rather than apologetic when cornered.

Personality

Initially hostile, arrogant and easily offended by scrutiny; meticulous about his craft and willing to ruin a client's reputation for personal gain.

Motivations

Turn privileged access to envoys into wealth, force lucrative clothing contracts through blackmail and keep his illicit listening work hidden behind professional prestige.

Adventure hook

An envoy receives a demand quoting a concession discussed only behind closed doors. She hires the party to inspect her new formal coat before tonight's negotiations. Charms beneath its lining relay speech to a locked workbox in Merrow's shop; matching stitches appear in the other envoys' coats. The party can disable the charms before the talks, recover the workbox and written demands as evidence, or feed Merrow a false concession to catch him selling it. He offers to destroy his notes if allowed to keep the payments already extorted.
