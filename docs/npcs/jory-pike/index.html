Session

Jory Pike

Human · Poacher · Fresh meat and borrowed excuses

Description

A poacher with a lame hunting dog beneath his stool who trades fresh meat for ale and keeps watch for the village reeve. A battered felt cap and patched coat suit a wiry man who knows every gap in the estate fence.

At the table

Check the door between sentences, tuck scraps aside for your dog, and call every deer a rabbit. Jory poaches to feed himself and his old hound, but his snares have lately caught someone else's attention.

Adventure hook

Someone is moving Jory's snares onto the public footpath, and a child has already been hurt. He can show the sites, but confronting the reeve would mean admitting whose traps they are.
