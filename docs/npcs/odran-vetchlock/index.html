Session

Odran Vetchlock

Human · Locksmith · Proof before the auction bell

Description

Odran repairs the town's locks in a cramped workshop, guarding each loose key as carefully as his purse. Balding and sharp-eyed, he greets unfamiliar customers with a demand for proof that they own whatever they want opened.

At the table

Cover a key with your palm and look from the visitor to the lock. Say, “Your door, is it? Then show me something besides confidence.” Keep replies clipped; a clear explanation and a witness earn a grudging hearing.

Personality

Initially hostile, suspicious and prickly about his reputation; he protects his customers' privacy even when they cannot pay promptly.

Motivations

Recover his missing master-key ring, replace compromised locks and keep innocent customers from being blamed for theft.

Adventure hook

Odran recognizes his stolen master-key ring in a lot of seized goods due to be auctioned in the market square at noon. The clerk refuses to release it without proof of ownership; Odran asks the party to fetch his numbered work ledger from the workshop storeroom and bring a watch officer to witness the claim while he keeps the bidding in sight.
