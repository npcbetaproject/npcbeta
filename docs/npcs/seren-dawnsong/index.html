Seren Dawnsong

Elf · Rebel mage · Freedom at a dangerous price

Description

Charismatic and reckless, Seren fights to free her city from an order she once belonged to. Ivory travelling robes, dark leather bracers and singed silver hair reveal a mage whose convictions have already cost her dearly.

At the table

Speak with infectious confidence. Treat cautious plans as challenges, but become quiet when civilians might bear the cost.

Adventure hook

She has stolen the key to the city’s magical defences, but using it could endanger the people she hopes to save.
