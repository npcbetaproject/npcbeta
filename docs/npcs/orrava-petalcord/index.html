Session

Orrava Petalcord

Orc · Flower bundler · Wedding flowers in a market squall

Description

Orrava ties bouquets at an open-air market stall, guarding her finished work with sharp looks and sharper instructions. A sudden squall threatens a delicate wedding order before its collectors arrive.

At the table

Keep tying stems, block any hand reaching toward a finished bundle and demand a clear offer of help. Say, “If you’re here to gawp, move. If you can carry a basket without crushing it, take that end.” She is initially hostile and impatient with interruptions.

Personality

Initially hostile, brusque and suspicious of careless help; meticulous about her promises and quick to protect another trader’s fragile stock as well as her own.

Motivations

Deliver the wedding flowers intact, pay the stall’s seasonal rent and prove that practical market flowers deserve the same care as a noble household’s arrangements.

Adventure hook

As rain drives beneath the awning, Orrava asks the party to help move six flower baskets to shelter. A neighboring trader has room beneath a covered loading arch but will not leave an unpaid cart unattended. The party can negotiate shared shelter, help move that cart first or find another dry route through the market. Orrava will accept a delayed collection rather than force anyone into an unsafe crossing. Keeping the flowers and the neighbor’s stock dry earns a dependable market contact, though her thanks remain blunt.
