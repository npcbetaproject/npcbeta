Session

Neris Claywhorl

Human · Potter · Mind the pots, and the tortoise

Description

Neris makes sturdy jars in a pottery yard and insists visitors try the handle before praising the glaze. Warm and eager to include newcomers, she offers a place at the workbench even when her own hands are full.

At the table

Smile, wipe your palms on your apron and offer someone a small useful job. Say, “Perfect timing, friend. Take that end, and I will keep the pots out of our way.” She thanks careful helpers by name and admits readily when a problem needs skills beyond hers.

Personality

Initially friendly, practical and encouraging; delighted by other people's expertise, but inclined to promise help before checking how much work she already has.

Motivations

Make affordable vessels that last, keep her shared yard welcoming and solve the day's trouble without an animal or neighbor paying for her haste.

Adventure hook

A cart-sized tortoise follows the warmth into Neris's yard and becomes wedged between her unlit kiln and a storage wall. A traveler claiming it arrives with a winch and wants to drag it through the narrow lane before dusk. Neris asks the party to move the pottery racks, coax the animal backward into the square and persuade the claimant to use the broad farm gate instead; she offers payment in coin or sturdy travel jars.
