Korra Needlefern

Orc · Dyer · Enough shade for both processions

Description

Korra dyes cloth for households and public celebrations, greeting customers with an armful of samples and an enthusiastic account of every shade. She remembers people's favorite colors and is quick to offer a practical kindness while talking through the job.

At the table

Spread cloth samples across the table, greet visitors by name once introduced and interrupt yourself to offer them a seat. Say, “Come closer! That blue looks different in the shade. Tell me what we are celebrating.” Her cheerful chatter becomes focused diplomacy when customers begin arguing.

Personality

Initially friendly, helpful and overly talkative; proud of her craft, quick to laugh at her own mistakes and determined that a celebration should not leave neighbors humiliated.

Motivations

Keep the communal canopy intact, honor the work stitched into it by earlier dyers and help both households enjoy their festival without a public quarrel.

Adventure hook

Two rival households claim the same communal canopy for processions starting at opposite ends of the market at noon. The steward proposes cutting it in two, which would split its old central picture panel. Korra asks the party to borrow a spare frame and plain canopy cloth from the traveling carnival, then persuade the household representatives to accept a shared schedule or the loan while she repairs the communal cloth's worn ties.
