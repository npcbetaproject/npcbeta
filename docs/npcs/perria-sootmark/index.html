Session

Perria Sootmark

Halfling · Ink maker · The terms nobody remembers

Description

Perria mixes writing inks and meets questions with a suspicious glare and clipped demands. She secretly sells a moneylender cursed contract ink that erases readers’ memories of the negotiations, letting the lender deny promises made before signing.

At the table

Keep one hand over the bottle stopper, challenge anyone who reaches across the counter and answer curiosity as though it were an accusation. Say, “Buying ink or prying? State the color and pay. My customers’ business is none of yours.” Her hostility is defensive and mocking, not an automatic attack.

Personality

Initially hostile, suspicious and quick to belittle unwelcome questions; deliberately exploits other people’s confusion while guarding the reputation of her ordinary inks.

Motivations

Collect a continuing cut of the lender’s proceeds, hide the curse among legitimate commissions and keep the uncursed drafts that make her indispensable to the scheme.

Adventure hook

An adult borrower remembers visiting a lender but cannot recall a promised grace period; two other clients describe the same gap after reading their contracts. Perria keeps the original uncursed drafts in her workshop, alongside orders linking the black bottles to the lender. Before a new group signs at dawn, the party can obtain those drafts, warn the prospective borrowers or persuade an affected clerk to compare the orders with the lender’s copies. Breaking the wax seal on Perria’s master bottle ends the curse on that batch and restores the lost negotiations. Perria offers to release the batch if the party lets her keep the evidence against the lender, creating a choice between immediate relief and a fuller public accounting.
