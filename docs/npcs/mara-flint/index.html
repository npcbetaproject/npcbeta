Session

Mara Flint

Human · Charcoal burner · Soot, silence and a single ale

Description

A charcoal burner who sits near the door, nursing one ale all evening and listening for work worth taking. Soot-smudged cheeks and a patched wool coat betray long days in the woods; she expects payment before promises.

At the table

Let others fill the silence, warm your hands around a cup, and ask what a job pays before anything else. Mara is saving to replace her cracked charcoal cart and will not risk it on a stranger's vague assurances.

Adventure hook

Mara saw a cart unload sacks at the abandoned limekiln after midnight, but nobody has bought fuel for it in months. She will describe the driver and route in exchange for help repairing her axle.
