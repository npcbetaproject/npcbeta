Session

Udrik Amberbark

Dwarf · Resin tapper · A marked batch beneath occupied rafters

Description

Udrik gathers and grades pine resin from a small woodland outpost, talking about yields while his hands remain busy. A batch he paid to store at an abandoned neighboring station is ready for collection, but nesting swallows now occupy the rafters above its loading hatch.

At the table

Check a bucket seal while visitors speak, ask what carrying gear they brought and answer in short practical phrases. Say, “Three marked tubs, no broken seals. Tell me how you’ll move them without wrecking the nests.” He is initially indifferent and focused on the job, not hostile to help.

Personality

Initially indifferent, absorbed in quantities and schedules; patient with careful work and unwilling to destroy a nesting colony merely to save himself a journey.

Motivations

Recover the paid-for resin intact, keep his own outpost supplied for repairs and leave the swallows undisturbed until their young have fledged.

Adventure hook

Udrik offers a share of his next resin delivery for help collecting three tubs bearing his pinecone mark. The usual loading hatch would disturb nests directly above it. The party can inspect the station with him, negotiate access through a neighboring forester’s side door or organize smaller loads through a clear passage. Udrik accepts delaying collection if no safe route is available, but wants the batch recorded and protected from weather in the meantime. A careful solution earns a reliable supply contact at his woodland outpost.
