Session

Della Wickfen

Dwarf · Candle maker · Every stub tells a story

Description

Della makes dependable candles and checks every returned stub before granting credit. Blunt and meticulous, she distrusts grand stories but keeps her promises down to the last copper.

At the table

Hold a taper up to the light before answering, ask for exact quantities, and correct exaggerations without raising your voice. Della wants to protect her apprentice and keep the workshop solvent; she offers practical help once someone earns her trust.

Adventure hook

Della finds a missing messenger’s signet ring embedded in a returned block of tallow and recognizes it from his regular visits. She hires the party to trace her supplier’s marked cart to a disused roadside shed before the remaining batch is melted at dawn; the messenger may still be held there.
