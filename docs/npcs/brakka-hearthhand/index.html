Brakka Hearthhand

Half-orc · Tavern Cook · Return the containers

Description

Broad-shouldered and flour-dusted, Brakka packs hearty travelling meals and takes fierce pride in her cooking. Gruff and practical, she judges adventurers by whether they return her containers.

At the table

Fold your arms, ask how many days of food they need, and tuck an extra portion into the bundle. Brakka quietly cares about her customers and wants her missing supplier brought home.

Adventure hook

A supplier’s delivery never arrived. Its cart was found near an old ruin with the food untouched and the driver missing; Brakka asks the party to follow the tracks.
