Session

Vessa Linenrook

Halfling · Awning maker · A safe way out from under canvas

Description

Vessa repairs and rents canvas awnings in an open-air market, treating interruptions as jobs to be priced and scheduled. A shifted canopy pole has trapped a delivery cart beneath sagging canvas, and she needs the market cleared before she can make a safe repair.

At the table

Keep measuring while visitors speak, ask who will pay and give a practical answer without pleasantries. Say, “Two signatures, clear tables and someone to hold the ladder. Then I can start.” She is busy and indifferent, not contemptuous.

Personality

Initially indifferent, task-focused and economical with words; patient with useful assistance and firm about stopping work when bystanders are in danger.

Motivations

Finish a sound repair before the storm, protect the stallholders’ livelihoods and persuade the market stewards to keep delivery lanes clear.

Adventure hook

Before afternoon rain, Vessa needs helpers to move customers away from a sagging market canopy and clear access to a delivery cart trapped beneath it. The adult driver refuses to abandon several fragile crates, while neighboring sellers worry about rain spoiling their stock. Vessa directs the repair once the area is clear. The party can organize an orderly unloading, find temporary covers or persuade the stewards to open a sheltered storage room. She offers a repair to the party’s travel gear in return and insists that nobody crawl beneath the damaged canopy.
