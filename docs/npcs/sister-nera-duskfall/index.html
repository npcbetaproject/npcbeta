Session

Sister Nera Duskfall

Human · Cleric · Bruised, certain, and still hunting

Description

Sister Nera Duskfall is found deep in the darkened ruins, following a vampire’s trail despite a bruised cheek and split lip. Her lantern is steady, her confidence unshaken, and she insists the creature can still be stopped before dawn.

At the table

Raise the lantern to inspect a threshold, wipe at the split lip without breaking her train of thought, and speak as if the next step is already settled. Nera welcomes capable help, but will not let caution become delay.

Personality

Calm, warm, and certain under pressure, she meets pain with a wry smile and refuses to dramatize the danger.

Motivations

She wants to find the vampire before dawn drives it toward the nearby settlement.

Adventure hook

Nera’s trail ends at a sealed crypt door; from behind it, a voice calls her by a private childhood name used only by her missing mentor. She asks the party to help decide whether the vampire is baiting her or needs help before dawn closes their chance.
