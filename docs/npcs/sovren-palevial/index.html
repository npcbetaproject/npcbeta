Session

Sovren Palevial

Elf · Physician · The patient never quite recovers

Description

Sovren serves an adult noble household with a distracted, strictly professional manner. He secretly prolongs his patient’s illness and uses exclusive control of the sickroom to sell access to the estate’s decision-maker.

At the table

Close the case before answering, check the hour and ask visitors to state their business briefly. Say, “Name, purpose and length of visit. I have other duties, and the patient requires quiet.” His initial indifference is brisk routine rather than overt intimidation.

Personality

Initially indifferent, preoccupied and transactional; privately controlling and willing to harm a dependent patient to preserve his influence.

Motivations

Keep the noble dependent on his care, collect payments from petitioners seeking private audiences and prevent an independent healer from examining the patient.

Adventure hook

A household steward asks the party to escort an independent healer after noticing that the noble improves whenever Sovren is away. The patient’s own letters request another opinion, but Sovren withholds them and claims all visits are forbidden. The party can recover the letters from his locked case, persuade the household to honor the patient’s wishes or bring the healer through a service passage with the steward’s help. Sovren’s private payment ledger links paid audiences to concessions granted during the illness. Confronted with evidence, he offers the party a share of the fees or threatens to accuse the steward of poisoning; securing the patient’s consent and independent care breaks his leverage.
