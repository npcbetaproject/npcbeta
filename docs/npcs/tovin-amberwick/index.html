Session

Tovin Amberwick

Gnome · Potion Vendor · Surviving brings repeat business

Description

An energetic merchant, Tovin arranges his counter into practical bundles of healing, light, antidotes and escape supplies. He asks where adventurers are going before recommending a purchase.

At the table

Line up bottles as you speak and ask about the route, the hazards and the budget. Say, “Surviving is excellent for repeat business.” Tovin values useful advice over an easy sale.

Adventure hook

A returned potion bottle contains something Tovin never sold. Its former owner insists the contents changed underground; Tovin asks the party to investigate the source before he restocks.
