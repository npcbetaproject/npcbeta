Session

Ivo Fernstep

Halfling · Falconer · A returning hawk and a missing courier

Description

Ivo trains hawks in an estate's mews and welcomes visitors with a spare glove and an invitation to watch. His chestnut curls never stay tidy, and he explains each bird's habits as readily as another person might introduce old friends.

At the table

Offer a safe place to stand and lower your voice when a bird settles. Smile and say, “Come closer, friend—slowly. She likes a quiet introduction, same as anyone.” Ask a visitor's name, remember it and patiently demonstrate instead of scolding mistakes.

Personality

Initially friendly, helpful and eager to share his craft; he notices nervousness and makes room for people to learn.

Motivations

Find the missing courier and keep his hawks safe while helping the search.

Adventure hook

Ivo's escaped hawk returns with a freshly cut jess and blue courier cloth tangled in its anklet. He last saw the bird circling a rock shelter off the mountain trail, where couriers shelter from rain; he asks the party to search it with him before the approaching storm washes away any tracks.
