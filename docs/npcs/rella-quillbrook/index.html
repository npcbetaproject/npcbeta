Session

Rella Quillbrook

Halfling · Traveling interpreter · Read back every word

Description

Rella travels between market towns, interpreting contracts and testimony for people far from home. Exacting and outspoken, she insists on reading every agreement back in both languages, even when an impatient official tells her to hurry.

At the table

Repeat the important word in each language, tap a pencil against the relevant line, and ask speakers to finish before translating. Rella corrects powerful people as readily as anyone else, but softens her tone for someone frightened of making a mistake.

Personality

Precise, forthright and quietly mischievous; she enjoys puncturing pomposity with an exact translation.

Motivations

She wants travelers judged by what they actually say and hopes to train enough interpreters that no one must sign words they cannot read.

Adventure hook

At a watchhouse, Rella discovers that a foreign carter's signed confession is actually a request for an interpreter. The clerk orders the prisoner transferred at sunset; she asks the party to find the original arrest witness at the caravanserai and bring them before the watch captain before the cart leaves.
