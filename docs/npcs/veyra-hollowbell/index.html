Session

Sister Veyra Hollowbell

Vampire · Mortuary keeper · The bell is for the living

Description

Veyra keeps a mortuary beside a neglected crypt, recording each delivery in a locked burial register. Pale, severe and openly suspicious of interruptions, she demands a name and a purpose before admitting anyone past the threshold.

At the table

Keep your chin still, watch a visitor’s throat and close the register when questioned. Say, “The mourners have gone. Explain why you have not.” Offer precise bargains when cornered, and let irritation slip when someone rings her small tarnished bell.

Personality

Initially hostile, controlling and contemptuous of curiosity. Her sinister work is deliberate: she conceals living captives among sealed funeral deliveries and feeds on them beneath the mortuary.

Motivations

Keep her feeding route concealed, silence witnesses and retain the official-looking register that makes her deliveries appear legitimate.

Adventure hook

A porter hears a living voice from a funeral cart and asks the party to inspect Veyra’s next delivery before midnight. Her register lists captives as buried strangers, and a loose page identifies the sealed crypt where they are held. The party can steal the crypt key from her bell cord, force her to exchange it for the incriminating register, or interrupt the cart at the crypt entrance. Veyra attacks if escape and bargaining fail; any deal leaves a predator who still needs to be stopped.
