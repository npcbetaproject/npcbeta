Session

Oren Sedgewhistle

Elf · Irrigation ditch keeper · A fair turn of water

Description

Oren clears farm irrigation ditches and remembers every household’s allotted turn of water. Cheerful and reluctant to take sides, he talks through disputes while quietly measuring whether anyone has taken more than their share.

At the table

Whistle off-key, mark water levels on a notched stick, and ask after the crops before discussing payment. Oren wants a fair water supply but avoids confrontation; firm allies help him speak up when his careful records are challenged.

Adventure hook

Oren finds a cut sluice rope and a weighted sack deliberately wedged in the channel supplying the lower farms. He asks the party to escort him upstream, clear the blockage and catch whoever returns to reset it before angry farmers smash the upstream mill’s wheel at sunset.
