Session

Mosswick Reedjaw

Troll · Sluice keeper · A flood held behind one missing crank

Description

Mosswick maintains a watermill sluice in exchange for flour and a dry shed. This broad, moss-skinned troll welcomes visitors with an eager grin and explains every repair as though he has finally found an interested apprentice.

At the table

Lean on an imaginary lever, beam at practical questions and praise a sound knot. Say, “Lovely timing! Hold this steady and I will show you which field we are saving.” His welcome hardens if someone forces the gate before the downstream path is cleared.

Personality

Initially friendly, enthusiastic and proud of useful work; protective of the people below the mill, but too ready to assume everyone understands the machinery.

Motivations

Keep the lower fields dry, preserve his arrangement with the miller and prove that his strength is good for more than frightening travellers.

Adventure hook

Someone steals the removable sluice crank and leaves enormous footprints pointing toward Mosswick’s shed. Water is backing up toward the lower fields, and a grain merchant offers a bounty for driving him away. Mosswick asks the party to follow the tracks to a reed-hidden handcart and recover the crank; the merchant has staged the theft to buy flooded farms cheaply. Clearing the downstream footpath and fitting the crank earns safe passage and Mosswick’s help, while trying to smash the gate makes him block the party by force.
