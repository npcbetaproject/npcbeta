Borun Claystep

Orc · Bathhouse keeper · Clean towels, troubled water

Description

Borun runs a modest bathhouse where muddy travelers receive clean towels and cheerful instructions about washing their boots. Fussy about cleanliness and generous with his time, he remembers which guests need a quiet bench more than conversation.

At the table

Fold a towel while listening, straighten a crooked stool, and offer practical comfort before asking questions. Borun laughs easily until someone treats his workers as expendable; then his voice becomes very quiet.

Personality

Sociable, orderly and protective, with a brisk kindness that leaves little room for self-pity.

Motivations

He wants his workers safe and the bathhouse affordable for the laborers who depend on it.

Adventure hook

Borun's apprentice is trapped behind a jammed grille in the bathhouse cistern, and the water is rising. Borun asks the party to reach the inlet valve through an old service passage while he holds the grille clear of floating debris; a contractor's dumped rubble has blocked the normal access.
