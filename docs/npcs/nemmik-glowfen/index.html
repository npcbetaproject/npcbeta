Session

Nemmik Glowfen

Gnome · Lamplighter · A light borrowed until moonrise

Description

Nemmik tends the market lamps and greets late shoppers with cheerful advice. With the oil delivery overdue, a willing will-o’-the-wisp lights his hand lantern under a promise that he will escort it home by moonrise.

At the table

Beam at newcomers, offer directions before they ask and address the light as a colleague. Say, “Glad you’re here! My bright friend has kept its promise; now I need to keep mine. Can you lend us a pair of hands?”

Personality

Initially friendly, eager to help and prone to explaining every practical detail; treats promises to unusual neighbors as seriously as contracts with townsfolk.

Motivations

Keep the market safe after dusk, repay the wisp’s freely offered help and earn enough trust to borrow emergency supplies next time.

Adventure hook

The market’s last reserve oil is locked in a store whose keeper is stranded beside a broken cart on the marsh boardwalk. Nemmik offers his evening’s pay for help bringing the keeper back before moonrise, when he must escort the willing wisp home. The party can recover the keeper and unlock the reserve, or negotiate a short extension with the wisp while arranging another light source. Keeping the wisp against its wishes would break Nemmik’s promise and cost the market a useful neighbor.
