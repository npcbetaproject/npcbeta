Session

Osric Rutledge

Human · Stagecoach Driver · The road owes you nothing

Description

Weather-beaten Osric drives a patched stagecoach with the caution of a man who has buried friends along the route. A former sailor, he calls passengers his cargo, horses his crew and every steep descent a bad stretch of sea.

At the table

Speak in a gravelly mutter, squint at the weather and punctuate advice with a dry chuckle. Osric complains about passengers but counts every head before moving off. He wants to finish his final season with everyone delivered safely, and will turn back rather than risk an exhausted team.

Adventure hook

At the next change station, a fresh detour notice directs coaches onto an abandoned toll road. Osric spots that its official seal is wrong. He offers the party passage if they scout the regular route and discover who wants travellers diverted.
