Session

Olyra Flintpetal

Elf · Basket weaver · A quiet way through the reeds

Description

Olyra weaves sturdy baskets at a market stall, giving customers precise prices while her hands keep working. She gathers her own reeds and treats delays as problems to solve rather than invitations to complain.

At the table

Test a reed between finger and thumb, leave a pause before answering and ask what the customer needs to carry. Say, “If you want the wide basket, come back tomorrow. If you know a quiet route through the marsh, we can discuss a trade.” Her initial indifference is concentration, not dislike.

Personality

Initially indifferent, practical and absorbed in her craft; patient with useful suggestions and careful about taking more from the marsh than it can spare.

Motivations

Fill her orders without wasting materials, preserve reliable reed beds and keep working access through agreements the marsh keeper can trust.

Adventure hook

Nesting cranes close the only firm reed-cutting bank just as Olyra needs material for a large fish-trap basket order. The marsh keeper will permit cutting on the far bank if the party scouts and marks a quiet punt route that avoids the nesting islets; the old channel stakes have sunk into the silt. Olyra offers finished baskets and a share of her fee for the survey. They can negotiate limited access to her already-cut bundles instead, but must agree how to carry them out without crossing the nests.
