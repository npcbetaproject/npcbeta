Session

Caldraven Duskmere

Vampire · Chamberlain · An emergency with no missing heir

Description

Caldraven manages a noble estate with detached precision and presents himself as its indispensable guardian. He has hidden letters proving the adult heir is alive and plans an emergency court oath that will bind the attending adults to his vampiric service, giving him control of the regency.

At the table

Check names against a seating plan, answer only the question asked and refer every interruption to the evening’s schedule. Say, “The heir’s absence is already accounted for. Sign in, take your assigned place and await the announcement.” His initial indifference is practiced administrative control.

Personality

Initially indifferent, formal and strictly professional; patient with procedure and chillingly certain that other people’s freedom is an acceptable price for a stable household.

Motivations

Become the estate’s permanent regent after decades of preserving it through sieges, afraid the returning heir will dismiss him. Keys, intercepted correspondence, household influence and loyal night servants support his scheme. He needs the senior steward’s freely given approval to open the emergency court and cannot compel that approval without invalidating the oath.

Adventure hook

The senior steward quietly asks the party to locate a courier who arrived with a letter from the supposedly lost heir. GM secret: Caldraven concealed that letter in his locked office and intends to turn an emergency oath into supernatural servitude. Before the evening court, the party can find the courier’s delivery receipt, recover the letter, secure the steward’s refusal to convene or persuade courtiers to leave. Caldraven offers records of the estate’s old enemies if allowed to retain his post, but insists on controlling the household. He may order his night servants to detain anyone removing the letter or breaking up the court; public proof that the heir lives and the steward’s withheld approval dismantle his pretext and stop the oath.
