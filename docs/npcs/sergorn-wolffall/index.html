Sergorn Wolffall

Human · Wilderness guide · The steady pathfinder

Description

Quiet and reliable, Sergorn knows when to keep moving and when the woods are asking travelers to stop. An olive cloak, worn leather gear and an attentive expression suit a guide who earns trust through careful preparation.

At the table

Listen before answering. Point out tracks and weather changes with few words; check everyone’s gear without making a fuss.

Adventure hook

Fresh tracks circle a remote camp but never approach or leave it. Sergorn needs companions to find out what is watching.
