Session

Sarra Hearthsedge

Halfling · Midwife · The road beyond the landslide

Description

Sarra travels between outlying households with clean linen, a worn satchel and a ready welcome. She learns visitors' names quickly and treats an offer of help as the beginning of a friendship.

At the table

Make room on the bench, ask after the visitor's household, and thank people for small practical kindnesses. Say, “Come sit, dear. Tell me what the road is like, and we will find a way.” Her warmth stays steady when plans need changing.

Personality

Initially friendly, warmly attentive and encouraging; practical about difficult journeys and firm about respecting the privacy of the families she visits.

Motivations

Reach the households relying on her, train a reliable travelling assistant and keep isolated neighbors connected to help.

Adventure hook

A landslide blocks Sarra's route to an expectant parent staying at an isolated farmhouse. The family has asked her to arrive before nightfall, but the only remaining path ends at a ferry landing whose keeper closes at dusk. She asks the party to check the old river path for washouts, arrange a crossing and escort her mule; she can pay modestly and introduce them to households along the route.
