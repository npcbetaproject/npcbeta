Session

Edda Appleturn

Gnome · Cider presser · One tree, another harvest

Description

Edda presses cider in a small orchard shed, greeting visitors with a cup and a cheerful account of the day's apples. She eagerly finds newcomers a useful job and remembers anyone who handles her old press with care.

At the table

Offer a seat, explain one small task and praise a careful attempt before correcting it. Say, “Come in, friend. Taste this batch, then tell me which job your hands would like.” Her chatter slows when she speaks about the old trees, but she still welcomes practical advice.

Personality

Initially friendly, generous and talkative; proud of patient craft and quick to trust someone who treats living things gently.

Motivations

Keep the village supplied with good cider, pass her knowledge to willing learners and preserve an old apple variety that her neighbors cannot replace by buying new trees.

Adventure hook

A storm splits the last tree of Edda's treasured cider-apple variety, leaving its healthy shoots high in unstable branches. She asks the party to help the orchard gardener salvage cuttings and carry them to a sheltered estate courtyard before the night's hard frost. The estate's cold frames stand empty, but its steward wants a repair deposit before granting access; Edda offers the party coin and next harvest's first barrel if they can arrange shelter and transport without losing the shoots.
