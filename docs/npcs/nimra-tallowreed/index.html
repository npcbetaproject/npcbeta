Session

Nimra Tallowreed

Gnome · Bookbinder · A page out of place

Description

Nimra binds ledgers and repairs worn books in a narrow shop smelling of paste and linen. Absorbed in a stubborn spine, she acknowledges visitors with a raised finger and finishes the stitch before hearing their business.

At the table

Keep your eyes on the binding, count stitches under your breath, and ask visitors to clear a dry patch of bench. Say, “One moment. Is this a repair, a purchase, or something that cannot wait?” Specific facts earn her attention; dramatic introductions do not.

Personality

Initially indifferent, precise and quietly wry; she revises an opinion readily when shown sound evidence.

Motivations

Expose the ledger's alteration before it harms someone, keep her workshop solvent and protect customers' private records.

Adventure hook

An estate clerk returns a rent ledger Nimra repaired, claiming it contains no record of a tenant family's payment. Nimra recognizes that the numbered pages jump across the binding: the payment folio was removed after delivery. She asks the party to witness the gap, recover yesterday's discarded papers from the estate storeroom and bring the evidence to the steward before tomorrow's eviction hearing.
