Session

Ulren Threadwake

Elf · Banner painter · A quiet audience for a stolen name

Description

Ulren paints festival banners in a riverside open-air market and discusses commissions with detached professionalism. He secretly plans to put the market’s audience to sleep so his hired attendants can abduct the adult witness who knows he stole his late patron’s inheritance.

At the table

Inspect a pigment sample, ask for dimensions and pause only to record a delivery address. Say, “Size, colors and the day you need it. Approval is due before I hang anything.” His indifference makes an illicit commission sound like ordinary work.

Personality

Initially indifferent, composed and strictly transactional; hungry for recognition as an independent artist, yet willing to strip others of their freedom to protect a reputation built on theft.

Motivations

Keep the inherited workshop and secure a prestigious festival commission before the witness’s public testimony. Years of unpaid work for his patron explain his grievance without excusing the theft or abduction. Paid attendants, access to the hanging crew and a borrowed enchanted pigment enable his scheme; his dependence on the pigment’s owner leaves evidence he cannot control.

Adventure hook

An adult market attendant asks the party why Ulren has ordered a covered cart for one passenger after the banner unveiling. GM secret: the sleeping curse needs the enchanted central banner on display; removing or covering it prevents the spell, and ordinary paint has no such effect. A loan receipt identifies the pigment’s owner, who insists it was lent only for a private demonstration and will demand its return if told the plan. Before the unveiling, the party can warn and protect the witness, stop the banner being displayed, recover the receipt or persuade an attendant to testify. Ulren offers the stolen inheritance records to keep his workshop, but the witness must remain free to speak.
