Session

Veyra Thornhush

Dryad · Orchard guardian · No axe beyond the wall

Description

Veyra tends an old orchard whose fruit feeds the neighboring farms, guarding its boundary with a pruning hook and a sharp tongue. She blocks strangers at the wall and assumes any wagon carries an axe until its owner proves otherwise.

At the table

Stand across the path, study hands before faces and answer grand introductions with a curt question. Say, “Another grand promise. Show me what is under that canvas.” She hears a practical bargain out, but refuses flattery; lighting a fire inside the orchard or cutting a living tree turns her warning into force.

Personality

Initially hostile, suspicious and mocking of careless promises; exact about boundaries and willing to honor a clearly stated agreement.

Motivations

Keep the living orchard intact, preserve the farms' shared harvest and secure a fuel arrangement that does not require destroying healthy trees.

Adventure hook

A charcoal crew arrives with paid cutting rights that place the orchard's oldest trees outside its protected boundary. Veyra seizes their tools and bars the cart track; the crew threatens to return with hired guards at dusk. She will release the tools if the party brokers access to fallen timber and gets the disputed boundary inspected. If anyone starts felling the living trees while talks continue, she will physically drive them out.
