Session

Tavrith Emberquill

Gnome · Fireworks maker · A bright display to hide a dark account

Description

Tavrith supplies festival displays with a bored, strictly transactional manner. She secretly helps a grain thief and plans to burn a warehouse during the celebration, destroying the storage records that expose their theft.

At the table

Check the sample case clasp, answer questions without looking up and quote a fee before discussing changes. Say, “Date, budget and where the audience stands. I don’t need the story behind the celebration.” Her indifference conceals calculation rather than open aggression.

Personality

Initially indifferent, busy and sparing with words; ruthlessly practical about concealing her complicity and willing to endanger neighbors to protect her share.

Motivations

Erase the grain trail before the warehouse audit, keep her illicit payment and retain a respectable trade after the theft is forgotten.

Adventure hook

An adult warehouse porter asks the party why Tavrith has paid for the building to be empty during the festival even though no display is booked there. A canceled delivery note and the warehouse tally book connect her patron to grain that never reached the public stores. Before the celebration, the party can secure those records, warn the watch and move spectators away, or persuade the porter to testify and force a public inventory. Tavrith offers to identify the buyer if the party helps recover her stolen share, but the immediate priority is stopping the planned fire and keeping the grain and witnesses safe.
