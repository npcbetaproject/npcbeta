Elmir Valebrook

Gnome · Merchant · A fair measure and a better story

Description

Cheerful and persuasive, Elmir can make a humble purchase feel like a lucky discovery. Copper curls, a plum coat and a small brass balance scale accompany a merchant who takes pride in finding the right goods for the right buyer.

At the table

Ask what the customer needs before naming a price. Tell a short story about each item, and weigh goods with deliberate care.

Adventure hook

A consignment arrived with one sealed parcel that appears on no invoice. Someone is offering far too much for it.
