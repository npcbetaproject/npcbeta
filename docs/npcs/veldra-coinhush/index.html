Session

Veldra Coinhush

Gnome · Auctioneer · The owner is inconveniently alive

Description

Veldra conducts estate auctions with brisk precision, giving every question the same flat attention as a disputed bid. Behind her orderly sales, this gnome profits from a forged death notice and an owner she intends to silence.

At the table

Straighten an inventory sheet, quote the next bid and treat interruptions as scheduling problems. Say, “If you dispute the sale, bring the owner before the bell. Otherwise, shall we proceed?” Keep her initial attitude indifferent even when her hidden agenda is exposed; she bargains in costs and consequences.

Personality

Initially indifferent, procedural and impatient with digressions; calculating and remorseless when another person's rights obstruct her profit.

Motivations

Collect the proceeds from a living traveler's estate, keep the fraud from being exposed and purchase influence with the stolen wealth.

Adventure hook

A porter recognizes a distinctive traveling chest in Veldra's sale inventory: he carried it for the supposedly dead owner at the caravanserai yesterday. He asks the party to find the traveler before tomorrow's auction. The owner is alive, but Veldra has hired attackers to prevent his return. The party can escort him to halt the sale and secure her false commission and payment order from the auction office. If confronted privately, Veldra offers a share of the proceeds for their silence; warning the owner also puts them in the attackers' way.
