Session

Thessra Thornmaze

Dryad · Estate maze steward · Negotiators lost until the treaty is signed

Description

Thessra manages a noble estate’s living maze and discusses its paths with cool formality. She has trapped visiting negotiators inside shifting hedges and will release them only after they recognize the maze as her private fey domain.

At the table

Consult the garden plan while visitors speak, quote an exit condition and discuss objections as delays in a transaction. Say, “The path opens when the terms are settled. Tell me which clause you are prepared to accept.” Her initial indifference masks deliberate captivity.

Personality

Initially indifferent, controlled and strictly transactional; she treats other people’s freedom as a bargaining tool and values the wording of a promise more than its humane purpose.

Motivations

Establish a lasting fey domain independent of her aging heart-tree, which has begun to hollow. Fear of ending with that tree drives her to force recognition from human negotiators, though she understands that they never consented to become her hostages.

Adventure hook

An estate servant hires the party after negotiators fail to return from a garden meeting. GM secret: Thessra keeps their routes looping until they sign a private treaty granting her authority over the maze. Animated hedge hounds patrol its junctions, and a sympathetic land agent has supplied the draft. The party can recover that draft, ask the agent to withdraw support or enter under the carved safe-conduct tokens issued at the gate. Her weakness is a promise she cannot evade: each token names a bearer entitled to leave at will, and speaking that right at the central arbor obliges the maze to open a path for that bearer. The party can secure tokens for the captives, negotiate a voluntary boundary agreement after their release or expose the confinement to the estate. Thessra offers access to hidden fey paths for recognition, but orders her hounds to intercept anyone cutting through occupied hedges or removing the treaty. Her scheme ends when the captives can leave and recognition is no longer coerced; destroying her tree is unnecessary.
