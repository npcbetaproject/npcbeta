Session

Bess Crowley

Human · Washerwoman · Nothing washes out a good rumour

Description

A washerwoman with cracked hands and a vicious sense of humour who knows whose blood she scrubbed out this morning. A plain kerchief, faded burgundy dress and worn apron accompany a regular who can puncture a boast with one sentence.

At the table

Dry your hands on your apron, deliver insults without raising your voice, and become brisk when someone is genuinely frightened. Bess protects her customers' privacy, but keeping quiet about this morning's laundry is beginning to trouble her.

Adventure hook

A local carter paid Bess double to clean a bloodied coat before his wife saw it. When she learns that the miller's apprentice is missing, she asks the party to check his yard before she names him in public.
