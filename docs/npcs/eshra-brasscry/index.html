Session

Eshra Brasscry

Harpy · Court herald · A disputed message at the court gate

Description

Eshra controls announcements at a royal court gatehouse, checking seals and delivery terms with detached precision. She blocks an unannounced messenger whose disputed letter could provoke a confrontation in the audience hall.

At the table

Glance past visitors toward the next scheduled arrival, repeat the required terms without warmth and leave pauses for a clear answer. Say, “Name, seal, and intended recipient. Agree to an escorted delivery, and I can find you a place.” She is initially indifferent and focused on her duty.

Personality

Initially indifferent, professional and easily bored by appeals to status; scrupulous about giving rival envoys the same hearing and unwilling to let impatience endanger the court.

Motivations

Keep the audience orderly, establish who accepts responsibility for the disputed message and finish her announcements without becoming a faction’s private enforcer.

Adventure hook

A messenger asks the party to get past Eshra before the next audience begins. She offers an escorted delivery, a private meeting with the recipient or verification by a mutually accepted witness; she does not require the letter to be read aloud. The party can establish the seal’s origin, persuade the messenger to accept a safe arrangement or negotiate a later hearing. If they force the gate or threaten another envoy, Eshra uses beating wings and hooked talons to drive them back rather than pursue retreating opponents. A verified delivery and agreed escort end the standoff and earn a reliable court contact.
