Session

Talvo Sedgepin

Gnome · Itinerant dentist · An appointment outside the walls

Description

Talvo carries her dental tools between market towns in a small covered wagon. Busy and strictly professional, she checks her appointment list before acknowledging a newcomer and expects payment terms settled before work begins.

At the table

Adjust your spectacles, count folded cloths and ask one clear question at a time. Say, “I have a patient waiting. Can you arrange a quiet space, or shall I ask someone else?” Keep the same level voice with a mayor or a giant; her patient is a person with an appointment, not a spectacle.

Personality

Initially indifferent, businesslike and hard to distract; meticulous about privacy and quietly stubborn when others obstruct her work.

Motivations

Earn a dependable living on the road and see every agreed patient without a crowd interfering.

Adventure hook

A giant with a painful tooth arrives for Talvo's promised appointment, but the guards refuse to let him through the town gate. She hires the party to arrange a quiet treatment space at the caravanserai, obtain the keeper's consent and bring her water and equipment from the market; curious onlookers are already gathering, and the embarrassed patient threatens to leave if he becomes an attraction.
