Torren Reedbank

Human · Fisherman · The catch that got away

Description

Weathered and sociable, Torren boasts about enormous catches and knows every bend in the river. His voice drops when he describes shapes moving through the woods across the water.

At the table

Spread your hands wider with each fishing story, then stop smiling when the opposite bank is mentioned. Torren wants his nets left alone and someone to believe what he saw.

Adventure hook

Something disturbs his nets at night. Yesterday he found one hanging high in a tree across the river; he asks the party to watch the bank while he checks the others.
