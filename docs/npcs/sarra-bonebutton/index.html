Session

Sarra Bonebutton

Goblin · Relic seller · A market for fear

Description

Sarra sells worn charms from a covered market stall and greets questions with a suspicious stare. She keeps a sealed bronze reliquary close, concealing a captive shade she intends to release when frightened customers will pay most for her wards.

At the table

Count a visitor's coins, shield the reliquary with your elbow and answer questions with a challenge. Say, “Buying, or just wearing out my patience? Keep your hands where I can see them.” Her bargains remain grudging even when she needs help, and she treats concern for bystanders as a weakness to exploit.

Personality

Initially hostile, defensive and contemptuous of curiosity. Her sinister agenda is deliberate: she means to endanger a crowd for profit and considers unprotected neighbors an acceptable cost.

Motivations

Stage a supernatural attack, sell her genuine protective pendants at panic prices and leave town before anyone links the shade to her stall.

Adventure hook

A buyer recognizes Sarra's bronze reliquary as stolen from a family crypt and asks the party to investigate before the evening market bell. Sarra plans to pull its engraved locking pin at the bell, releasing a shade that avoids anyone wearing one of her wards. The party can seize the reliquary with its pin still in place and deliver it to the watchhouse, or clear the square and expose her scheme to the watch; the matching crypt carving proves the vessel's purpose. Sarra offers stolen relics for silence if cornered.
