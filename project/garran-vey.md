# Garran Vey

Male human master swordsmith and proprietor of The True Edge, a town weapons shop specializing in finely balanced swords and custom commissions. Custom work is practical craftsmanship, not automatically magical.

## Character draft

Garran fits a weapon's grip, reach and balance to its buyer. Patient and courteous, he asks customers to demonstrate their grip with a wooden practice sword before discussing a commission. He would rather lose a sale than deliver poor work.

Counterfeit brittle swords bearing his copied maker's mark have reached the watch. A customer's receipt points to a warehouse expecting another shipment tomorrow. Garran asks the party to trace the seller before injuries or closure of his shop.
