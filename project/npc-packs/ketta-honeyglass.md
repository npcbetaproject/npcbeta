# Ketta Honeyglass

Single original Library NPC created for NPC Beta.

- **Ancestry / profession:** Goblin beekeeper
- **Concept:** Patient, practical and gently amused; her bees return with pollen from an unknown flower.
- **Table manner:** She listens before answering and names each queen's mood like a weather report.
- **Hook:** Her hives fall silent after moonrise; bees leave a wax key that points toward a shuttered apiary.
- **Filters:** Workers & Services; Settlements and Wilderness
- **Published locations:** None assigned.
