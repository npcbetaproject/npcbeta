# Deep roads and crooked deals

Created and publication authorized 2026-10-17. Three independent Library NPCs:

- Riggles Flintnose: deep gnome gem prospector and trader. His acute nose follows fresh ore dust and familiar scents; smoke and perfume interfere. Underground is an encounter setting, not a criminal occupation.
- Maera Cinderhand: dwarf weaponsmith who prizes safe, dependable work. Her hook traces a missing caravan guard through a repaired sword.
- Oswald Venn: human deputy reeve who extorts permit fees and accepts payments to admit stolen goods.

Artwork uses the built-in image generator with Osric Rutledge as the approved painted storybook style reference. Portrait prompts specify centered waist-up framing, full heads and ears, clear headroom, earthy colours and visible brushwork, with no text or extra figures. Subjects: Riggles sniffing violet ore in a lamplit mine, Maera with black braids, hammer and unfinished blade at her forge, Oswald with burgundy coat, seal and permit in a records office.

Each token was generated from its own finalized portrait, using Brakka's token frame and the site dragon as references: antique brass/burgundy/charcoal circle, dragon-only bottom medallion, complete head and transparent exterior. Portrait exports: 960px WebP quality75/method6. Tokens: 280px transparent WebP quality80/method6 with a safety margin. See the shared portrait/token standards for future work.

Content review clarified Maera's missing-guard hook. Shared token and public-page tests, classification/reference checks, asset checks and desktop/mobile browser checks cover this batch.
