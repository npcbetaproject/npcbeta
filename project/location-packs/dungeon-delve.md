# Dungeon Delve locations

Three independent location templates: Crypt (`crypt`), Alchemist’s Laboratory (`alchemists-laboratory`), and Cavern Pool (`cavern-pool`). Existing Burial Vault and all saved Session instances are preserved.

Each record includes a GM description, visual read-aloud sentence, two sensory flavour sentences, a discovery hook, filter settings, descriptive image alt text and a publication date for Most Recent sorting. Crypt and Laboratory are Abandoned; the natural Cavern Pool has no condition label.

## Artwork

Generated separately with the built-in image-generation tool. Final assets are opaque 960 × 540 WebP, quality 75, method 6, under `docs/images/locations/<id>.webp`.

Shared prompt: standalone wide 16:9 NPC Beta fantasy RPG environment illustration; visibly painterly rather than photographic, grounded natural textures, muted earthy colours, softly cinematic light and a readable room layout. Show the entire environment rather than a close-up prop. No people, prominent monsters, text, labels, borders or watermark.

- Crypt: ancient subterranean crypt, heavy stone coffins, stacked burial niches, displaced sarcophagus lid, scattered grave dust and fresh scrape marks. Vaulted weathered stone, sparse warm candlelight against cool shadows. No bodies or gore.
- Alchemist’s Laboratory: abandoned medieval underground laboratory, worn wooden workbench, broken vessels, copper distillation apparatus, spilled reagents, dusty notes without legible text and mixtures faintly glowing amber and green. Interrupted experiment; no modern laboratory equipment.
- Cavern Pool: natural limestone cavern, still blue-green pool, glowing fungi on damp rock shelves, stalactites and an accessible rough shoreline. A submerged carved stone arch hints at a secret passage beneath clear shallows; deeper water remains mysterious.
