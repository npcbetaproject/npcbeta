# Missing Name Generator locations

Five additions complete artwork coverage for the existing Name Generator choices. This is a coverage update, preserving the established three-location pack convention for future themed packs.

Final assets: `docs/images/locations/<id>-location.webp`, exactly 1920 × 1080, opaque WebP, quality 85. Images were created separately with the built-in image generation tool; generator cards reuse these files as grayscale backgrounds beneath the shared SVG silhouette. No combined NPC images are stored.

Each record includes `id`, `name`, `description`, `settings`, `condition`, `image`, `imageAlt` and `publishedAt`. Existing records, setting/condition labels and generator location choices are preserved. One shared publication timestamp places the five additions first under Most recent, retaining the following order.

Setting tags describe plausible placement using the existing five setting filters. A ship may be moored beside a settlement or sailing away from one; a carnival can set up within or outside settlements. These tags do not change the separate NPC Library Location Fit categories.

## Final prompt set

Shared prompt, followed by the corresponding scene below:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: NPC Beta fantasy location card and reusable generated NPC backdrop. Create one standalone detailed realistic fantasy environment illustration. Grounded medieval fantasy, natural wood and stone textures, muted earthy palette, soft cinematic lighting, cohesive RPG art style. Wide 16:9 landscape composition with the important setting also readable in a centered square crop. No people or humanoid figures, no prominent creatures, modern objects, text, labels, frames or watermark. Scene:

- **Wizard’s Tower:** A secluded round stone wizard's tower with a conical slate roof, small amber-lit arched windows, an exterior stair and an open study doorway revealing shelves and an astronomical instrument. Ancient forest and distant hills surround its stone foundation. Subtle magical blue light near one window, grounded believable architecture.
- **Ship:** The open deck of a medieval wooden sailing ship at a calm harbor, with mast, neatly furled canvas sails, rigging, a raised stern deck, cargo barrels and the sea and distant harbor visible beyond the rails. No crew. Broad deck foreground and readable nautical setting.
- **Mine / Quarry:** A worked rocky quarry with a timber-supported mine entrance, narrow ore cart rails, a small loaded cart, stacked cut stone and a simple wooden lifting frame. Rugged hills beyond, warm lanterns inside the mine, natural daylight across rough stone.
- **Academy / Library:** The interior of a medieval scholarly academy library, tall oak bookshelves, stone arches, wooden reading tables with closed leather books, parchment rolls and a brass armillary sphere. Soft daylight through high arched windows and a few candle lamps. Clear aisle into the background.
- **Traveling Carnival:** A small medieval traveling carnival set up on a grassy field beside a country road at dusk, burgundy and ivory canvas performance tents, timber wagons, pennant strings, lanterns and a modest outdoor wooden stage. No people, no modern amusement rides or electric lights. An inviting open path between tents.
