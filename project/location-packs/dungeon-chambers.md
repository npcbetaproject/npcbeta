# Dungeon Chambers

Three-location pack: Ritual Chamber, Forgotten Storeroom and Prison Cells.

Assets: `docs/images/locations/ritual-chamber.webp`, `forgotten-storeroom.webp`, `prison-cells.webp`; each 1920 × 1080, opaque WebP quality 90. Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Existing `prison-cells` template is preserved; the pack uses `dungeon-prison-cells` as its stable ID.

## Final prompts

Shared direction: One standalone detailed realistic fantasy illustration for an NPC Beta location card. Grounded medieval dungeon architecture, muted earthy palette, cinematic lighting, natural stone and wood texture, clear readable room layout. Wide 16:9 landscape composition, no people, text, labels or watermark. One of three coordinated dungeon interior assets.

- Ritual Chamber: Circular stone ritual chamber, carved arcane floor sigils, clusters of candles, central stone altar with faint violet light, empty place waiting beside altar.
- Forgotten Storeroom: Forgotten underground storeroom, dusty wooden crates and cracked barrels, crowded shelves, narrow aisle into shadow, warm hanging lantern, fresh footprints in dust.
- Prison Cells: Medieval underground prison cells, rusted iron bars, damp stone, wall shackles, abandoned guard desk, cold light from small high opening, one clean empty cell with a meal on a tray.
