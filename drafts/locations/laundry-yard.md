# Laundry Yard

Category: Trade & Services

Description: A shared work yard where clothes and linens are boiled, scrubbed, rinsed and hung to dry for households, inns and institutions.

Read aloud: Steam rises from copper wash kettles beside wooden troughs, while lines of sheets and work clothes sway above baskets piled with wet linen.

More flavour: Soap and hot linen scent the damp air as water runs steadily along a stone drain. Wooden paddles knock against the troughs, and clothespins click whenever the breeze pulls at the drying lines.

Discovery: One unclaimed sheet bears a repeated pattern of rust-coloured marks; when folded along its creases, they form a rough street map with a single doorway circled.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
