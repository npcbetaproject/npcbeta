# Scrivener’s Office

Category: Civic & Professional

Description: A public writing office where contracts, petitions, letters and certified copies are prepared for a fee.

Read aloud: Tall desks line a narrow office, each set with ink pots, sealing wax and stacks of documents weighted beneath smooth stones.

More flavour: Quills rasp steadily over parchment beneath the soft tick of a brass sand timer. The room smells of lampblack ink, warm wax and the dry dust of bundled records.

Discovery: One ledger has been rebound upside down, hiding a narrow pocket in its spine that holds an unsigned copy of a recently altered contract.

Setting: Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
