# Wheelwright’s Workshop

Category: Trade & Craft

Description: A workshop where hubs, spokes and curved felloe segments are shaped and assembled into cart and wagon wheels before iron tyres are fitted.

Read aloud: An unfinished wagon wheel stands clamped between heavy timber posts, surrounded by shaped spokes, curved rim segments, iron tyres and benches covered in hand tools.

More flavour: Fresh oak and hot iron scent the open workshop as pale shavings gather beneath the benches. Mallets strike spoke tenons in an even rhythm while a newly fitted tyre ticks as it cools.

Discovery: A freshly repaired wheel uses alternating old and new spokes, and scrape marks on the older wood form a sequence of symbols when the wheel is turned.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
