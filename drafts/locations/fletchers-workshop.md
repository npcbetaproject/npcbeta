# Fletcher’s Workshop

Category: Trade & Craft

Description: A workshop where wooden shafts are straightened, nocked and feathered into arrows for hunters, guards and travellers.

Read aloud: A long workbench stands beneath an open window, covered with smooth wooden shafts, pale feather bundles, measuring guides and a small iron fletching jig.

More flavour: Fresh-cut cedar and warm hide glue sharpen the smell of waxed thread. Feathers rustle in their baskets while a shaft turns with a soft scrape inside the fletching jig.

Discovery: A dozen finished arrows use the same dark feather on one side; when laid in order, the clipped edges form a simple route through the nearby streets.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
