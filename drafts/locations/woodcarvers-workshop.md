# Woodcarver’s Workshop

Category: Trade & Craft

Description: A workshop where timber is shaped with chisels and gouges into household wares, signs, toys and decorative panels.

Read aloud: Pale curls of wood cover a scarred workbench beneath racks of chisels, unfinished figures and carved panels.

More flavour: Fresh-cut cedar and beeswax scent the warm, dusty air. Fine shavings whisper across the floorboards whenever a breeze enters through the open door.

Discovery: The unfinished signboard clamped to the bench conceals a thin hinged panel, sized to hide a folded letter or small key.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
