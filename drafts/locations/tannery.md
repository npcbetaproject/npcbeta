# Tannery

Category: Trades & Workshops

Description: A leatherworker’s yard where raw hides are soaked, scraped, stretched and treated with bark liquor before being cut into durable leather.

Read aloud: Low timber sheds surround rows of soaking vats, scraping beams and hide-covered frames darkened by years of bark liquor.

More flavour: Sharp lye and wet hide sour the air beneath the warmer scent of oak bark. Water drips from stretched skins while wooden paddles knock against the rims of deep vats.

Discovery: One stretched hide bears a line of neat pinholes that forms a rough map when held to the light, with a single riverside bend marked twice.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
