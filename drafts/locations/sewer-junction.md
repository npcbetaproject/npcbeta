# Sewer Junction

Category: Settlement Infrastructure

Description: A junction of brick-lined drainage tunnels where runoff channels meet beneath a settlement, with narrow service ledges and sluice gates for maintenance.

Read aloud: Brick arches meet above three dark runoff channels, with narrow stone ledges circling an iron sluice gate and a ladder rising toward the street.

More flavour: Water murmurs through the channels, carrying the sour smell of wet stone and rust. Drops strike the ledges in a slow, uneven rhythm beneath the distant rumble of cart wheels.

Discovery: Fresh mortar seals a low side channel, but a steady draft slips through a fist-sized gap near the floor.

Setting: Town, City, Underground. Condition: Maintained.
