# Saltworks

Category: Coastal & Commerce

Description: A coastal worksite where seawater is channelled into shallow pans and evaporated, leaving salt to be raked, dried and packed for trade.

Read aloud: Low stone-lined pans gleam beneath the sun beside timber sluice gates, salt-crusted rakes and a weathered drying shed stacked with pale sacks.

More flavour: Brine stings your nose while a dry mineral tang settles on your lips. Wind rattles the sluice boards and skims thin crystals across the pan edges with a faint glassy hiss.

Discovery: A salt-caked rake has a hollow handle sealed with wax, holding a tightly rolled list of coded weights and dates.

Setting: Village, Town, Wilderness. Condition: Maintained.
