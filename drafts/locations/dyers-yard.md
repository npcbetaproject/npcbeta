# Dyer’s Yard

Category: Craft & Commerce

Description: A workshop yard where cloth and yarn are boiled in dye vats, rinsed and hung to dry before sale or further finishing.

Read aloud: Rows of brightly dyed cloth hang above steaming copper vats, wooden rinsing troughs and worktables stained in overlapping colours.

More flavour: Hot steam carries the sharp scent of vinegar, wet wool and crushed roots across the yard. Coloured water trickles through shallow channels while suspended fabric snaps softly in the breeze.

Discovery: One drying length shows a pale repeating pattern beneath its blue dye, revealing writing woven into the cloth that becomes visible only while wet.

Setting: Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
