# Watermill

Category: Rural & Wilderness

Description: A riverside workshop where a waterwheel powers millstones that grind grain into flour for nearby farms and settlements.

Read aloud: A broad wooden waterwheel turns beside a stone-and-timber mill, feeding a churning millrace beneath stacked grain sacks and a covered loading porch.

More flavour: The wheel groans steadily as wet paddles slap the current and gears thump behind the walls. Flour dust sweetens the damp air, settling in pale drifts across the threshold.

Discovery: A flour-dusted floorboard beside the main axle lifts to reveal a narrow compartment containing a bundle of tally sticks with several deliveries cut away.

Setting: Village, Wilderness. Condition: Maintained.
