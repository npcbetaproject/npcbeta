# Communal Bakehouse

Category: Food & Trade

Description: A shared baking house where households and bakers prepare dough and use a large communal oven, with worktables, proofing baskets and cooling racks.

Read aloud: A soot-dark brick oven glows beside broad flour-dusted tables, while proofing baskets and racks of cooling loaves fill the warm stone room.

More flavour: Warm bread and woodsmoke sweeten the air as the fire settles behind the oven mouth. Wooden peels scrape over brick, and linen cloths stir in the draft from the open shutters.

Discovery: A clay tally left beneath the balance scale bears the same simple notch pattern as three loaves set apart on the lowest rack.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
