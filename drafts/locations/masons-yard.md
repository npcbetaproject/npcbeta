# Mason’s Yard

Category: Trade & Craft

Description: An open work yard where stone is measured, cut and dressed into blocks, steps, lintels and carved fittings for local buildings.

Read aloud: Squared stone blocks stand around a timber lifting frame beside scarred workbenches covered with chisels, mallets and pale dust.

More flavour: Metal taps ring across the yard in a patient rhythm while grit crunches underfoot. The air smells of wet stone, chalk and the hemp ropes coiled beside the hoist.

Discovery: Several finished blocks carry a second set of mason’s marks beneath fresh chisel cuts, showing they were reclaimed from another building and passed off as new.

Setting: Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
