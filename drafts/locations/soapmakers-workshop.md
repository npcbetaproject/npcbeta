# Soapmaker’s Workshop

Category: Trade & Craft

Description: A workshop where ash lye and rendered oils are boiled, scented, poured into moulds and cut into bars for washing, laundry and trade.

Read aloud: A broad copper kettle steams above a brick hearth beside long wooden moulds, a wire cutting frame and shelves stacked with pale soap bars.

More flavour: Woodsmoke and warm oils mingle with lavender and rosemary in the humid air. Wooden moulds knock against the worktable while cooling bars creak softly on their racks.

Discovery: One crate is stamped as plain household soap, but the cut bars inside carry a different maker’s seal and a faint medicinal scent.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
