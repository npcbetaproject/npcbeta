# Bridge Tollhouse

Category: Travel & Infrastructure

Description: A roadside post where fees are collected from travelers and wagons crossing a maintained bridge.

Read aloud: A striped barrier blocks the muddy road beside a small tollhouse, where an open ledger rests above an iron cash box and a low stone bridge spans the river.

More flavour: River water murmurs beneath the bridge, carrying the smell of wet stone and trampled reeds. Loose coins chink inside the toll box whenever the barrier arm settles against its wooden stop.

Discovery: A page has been carefully removed from the toll ledger, but the next sheet retains impressions of several wagon entries recorded after midnight.

Setting: Village, Town, Wilderness. Condition: Maintained.
