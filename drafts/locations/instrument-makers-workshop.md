# Instrument Maker’s Workshop

Category: Trade & Craft

Description: A workshop where lutes, fiddles, harps and other wooden instruments are shaped, strung, repaired and tuned.

Read aloud: Lutes, fiddles, small harps and drums hang above a scarred bench where an unfinished instrument rests among clamps, chisels and curled shavings.

More flavour: The room smells of spruce resin, hide glue and beeswax polish. A newly strung fiddle gives a faint answering hum whenever a wagon rattles past outside.

Discovery: A strip of parchment is glued beneath the unfinished lute’s soundboard; fragments of a route and several dates can be read through the carved sound hole.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
