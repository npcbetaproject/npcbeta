# Cooperage

Category: Trades & Workshops

Description: A barrel-maker’s workshop where timber staves are shaped, steamed and bound with iron hoops into casks for storage and trade.

Read aloud: Curved oak staves, iron hoops and half-built casks crowd an open timber workshop around a steaming trough and scarred workbenches.

More flavour: Fresh shavings curl across the floor, filling the warm air with the scent of split oak. Wooden mallets thud against barrel hoops while steam hisses from the trough beside the yard.

Discovery: A completed cask sounds solid when struck except for one narrow stave, which conceals a rolled shipping tally inside a drilled channel.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
