# Bookbinder’s Workshop

Category: Trade & Craft

Description: A workshop where loose sheets are folded, sewn and bound into ledgers, devotional books and private volumes using presses, leather and glue.

Read aloud: Tall sewing frames and a heavy wooden press fill the workshop, surrounded by folded paper, trimmed leather, brass tools and books clamped between boards.

More flavour: Paste and warm hide glue sharpen the dry scent of paper and leather. Thread whispers through punched gatherings while the press screws creak under steady tension.

Discovery: A finished ledger has been rebound around an older set of pages, whose trimmed edges still show fragments of different handwriting.

Setting: Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
