# Brewery

Category: Food & Drink

Description: A working brewhouse where malted grain is mashed, boiled and fermented into ale, with a copper kettle, cooling troughs and rows of casks.

Read aloud: A broad copper kettle stands over a brick hearth beside steaming mash tubs, cooling troughs and casks stacked along the walls.

More flavour: Warm malt and woodsmoke sweeten the humid air. Somewhere behind the vats, a bung creaks as fresh ale works inside its cask.

Discovery: Scratched tally marks beneath one cask reveal that several finished barrels were moved without appearing in the brewer’s ledger.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
