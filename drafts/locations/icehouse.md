# Icehouse

Category: Food & Storage

Description: An insulated store where winter ice is packed beneath straw and sawdust to preserve food, medicine and drink through warmer months.

Read aloud: Timber bins filled with straw-packed ice occupy the frost-lined chamber beside a pulley hook, storage shelves and a narrow meltwater channel.

More flavour: Cold air settles close to the floor, carrying the clean scent of ice and damp stone. Meltwater trickles along the narrow drain while sawdust whispers from the packed timber bins.

Discovery: One cloudy ice block contains a waxed leather pouch frozen at its centre, visible only where the outer layer has begun to melt.

Setting: Village, Town, City, Wilderness. Condition: Maintained.
