# Saddler’s Workshop

Category: Trade & Craft

Description: A workshop where saddles, bridles and other riding tack are cut, stitched and fitted for horses, pack animals and their riders.

Read aloud: A half-finished leather saddle rests on a timber stand beside a scarred workbench, while bridles, reins and girths hang in orderly rows along the plastered wall.

More flavour: Warm leather, beeswax and charcoal scent the sunlit workshop. Buckles chime softly against the wall as waxed thread draws tight through a half-finished saddle.

Discovery: A saddle awaiting collection has an extra layer beneath its seat; inside, a narrow leather sleeve conceals a hand-drawn route marked with three isolated waypoints.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
