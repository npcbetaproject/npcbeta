# Basketmaker’s Workshop

Category: Trade & Craft

Description: A workshop where soaked willow, reeds and split cane are woven into baskets, creels, hampers and sturdy containers for local work and trade.

Read aloud: A half-woven willow basket sits clamped in a wooden jig beside bundled rods, a stone soaking trough and shelves crowded with finished wickerwork.

More flavour: Wet willow and fresh-cut reeds lend the workshop a green, earthy smell. Thin rods creak beneath steady pressure while loose lengths whisper against the rim of the soaking trough.

Discovery: One finished hamper has a second wall woven inside it, creating a slim hidden compartment around the base.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
