# Granary

Category: Food & Agriculture

Description: A raised storehouse where grain is dried, measured and kept in sealed bins before milling or winter distribution.

Read aloud: A broad timber granary stands on stone piers above a swept yard, its loading doors flanked by stacked sacks, handcarts and covered grain bins.

More flavour: Chaff drifts through slanting light, carrying the dry scent of wheat and cedar boards. Somewhere beneath the floor, a wooden rattle turns steadily in the breeze to discourage vermin.

Discovery: One measuring scoop has a false bottom hiding a strip of waxed cloth marked with repeated delivery dates and an unfamiliar seal.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
