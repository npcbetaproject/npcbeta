# Mapmaker’s Workshop

Category: Trade & Craft

Description: A workshop where survey notes are turned into maps for roads, estates, coasts and unfamiliar country.

Read aloud: An angled drafting table fills the workshop beneath a broad regional map held flat by brass weights, surrounded by dividers, rulers, quills and rolled charts.

More flavour: Dry parchment and lamp oil scent the room while brass dividers scratch softly across the drafting table. Rolled charts rustle in their pigeonholes whenever a breeze enters through the open window.

Discovery: A patched section of the largest map covers an older road that continues beneath the repair and ends at a landmark omitted from the finished copy.

Setting: Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
