# Potter’s Yard

Category: Trades & Workshops

Description: A clayworker’s workshop where local clay is wedged, shaped on foot-driven wheels, glazed and fired into useful vessels and tiles.

Read aloud: Rows of drying bowls and jugs line timber shelves around a soot-dark kiln, foot-driven wheels and tables dusted with pale clay.

More flavour: Damp earth and woodsmoke mingle beneath the sharper mineral scent of glaze. A slow wheel creaks somewhere in the yard while cooling pots tick softly inside the kiln.

Discovery: A loose kiln brick conceals a narrow cavity holding three palm-sized clay tablets impressed with the same unfamiliar route marks.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
