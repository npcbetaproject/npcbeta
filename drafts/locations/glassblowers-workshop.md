# Glassblower’s Workshop

Category: Craft & Commerce

Description: A specialist workshop where molten glass is shaped into bottles, lenses, beads and decorative wares around a fiercely heated furnace.

Read aloud: A round brick furnace glows at the center of a cramped workshop, surrounded by long iron pipes, a scarred worktable and shelves of coloured glass.

More flavour: Heat presses against your face, carrying the mineral tang of ash and hot metal. Cooling glass ticks softly on the shelves while the furnace roars beneath a haze of smoke.

Discovery: A cloudy blue bottle on the cooling rack contains a sealed scrap of vellum, though its narrow neck is too small for the rolled message to have been inserted after firing.

Setting: Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
