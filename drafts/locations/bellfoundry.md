# Bellfoundry

Category: Trade & Craft

Description: A workshop where bronze bells are moulded, cast, finished and tuned for temples, civic buildings, ships and livestock.

Read aloud: A clay bell mould rises from a casting pit beside a glowing brick furnace, while a finished bronze bell hangs from a heavy timber testing frame.

More flavour: Soot, hot brick and sharp metal scent the broad workshop. A wooden testing mallet taps the suspended bell, sending a low note trembling through the roof beams.

Discovery: Inside one finished bell’s crown, a second maker’s mark has been filed nearly smooth, though its unfamiliar outline remains visible in angled light.

Setting: Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
