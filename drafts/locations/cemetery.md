# Cemetery

Category: Community & Faith

Description: A communal burial ground enclosed by a low wall, with marked graves, family plots and paths kept by local caretakers.

Read aloud: A gravel path winds between rows of weathered headstones inside a low stone wall, beneath yew branches hung with pale ribbons.

More flavour: Damp earth and clipped rosemary scent the still air. A small iron bell beside the gate taps softly whenever the wind shifts.

Discovery: One marker has been turned to face the wall, exposing a second inscription cut across its hidden side.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
