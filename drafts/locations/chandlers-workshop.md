# Chandler’s Workshop

Category: Craft & Trade

Description: A workshop where wax and tallow are melted, wicks are dipped or moulded, and finished candles are trimmed and bundled for sale.

Read aloud: Rows of pale candles hang from wooden dipping frames beside a copper melting vat, wick reels and wax-streaked worktables.

More flavour: Warm beeswax scents the room while the vat murmurs over a low fire. Cooling candles creak faintly on their frames, and clipped wicks collect like straw beneath the benches.

Discovery: One finished candle is noticeably heavier than the others; beneath its final wax layers, a narrow metal tube holds a tightly rolled scrap of paper.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
