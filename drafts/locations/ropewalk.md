# Ropewalk

Category: Craft & Commerce

Description: A long, narrow workshop where flax, hemp and other fibres are spun, stretched and twisted into cordage for ships, farms and builders.

Read aloud: A roofed timber lane stretches between turning wheels and pegged racks, with pale fibres drawn into long strands above coils of finished rope.

More flavour: Dry hemp scratches beneath every step, filling the air with a grassy, dusty scent. Wooden hooks chatter in sequence as taut strands creak and tighten down the length of the shed.

Discovery: One coil carries a line of differently knotted fibres beneath its outer wrap, forming a compact route diagram when the rope is unrolled.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
