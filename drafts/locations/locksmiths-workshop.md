# Locksmith’s Workshop

Category: Trade & Craft

Description: A workshop where locks, keys, latches and secure fittings are designed, repaired and fitted to doors, chests and strongboxes.

Read aloud: Rows of iron keys hang above a scarred workbench where an opened lock case sits clamped beneath a bright window.

More flavour: Fine filings glitter across the wood, and warm oil sharpens the smell of iron and charcoal. Springs click softly as a half-finished mechanism settles beneath a testing pick.

Discovery: One drawer holds wax impressions of several keys, each marked only by a different notch cut into its edge.

Setting: Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
