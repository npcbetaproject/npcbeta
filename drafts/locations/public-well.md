# Public Well

Category: Settlement Infrastructure

Description: A communal water source with a stone curb, timber winch and nearby trough, often serving as a gathering place for residents and travellers.

Read aloud: A waist-high stone well stands beneath a shingled canopy, with a wooden bucket hanging from its winch beside a long watering trough.

More flavour: The pulley groans as the rope turns, and cool air rises carrying the mineral smell of deep water. Damp moss darkens the lower stones while small coins glint between the cracks.

Discovery: One coping stone lifts free, revealing a shallow cache containing a wax-sealed note and several mismatched keys.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
