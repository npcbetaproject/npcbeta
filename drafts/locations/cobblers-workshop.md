# Cobbler’s Workshop

Category: Trade & Craft

Description: A workshop where shoes and boots are cut, stitched, soled and repaired using wooden lasts, hand tools and worked leather.

Read aloud: A half-finished boot rests on a wooden last at the centre of a scarred bench covered with awls, hammers, thread and cut leather soles.

More flavour: Beeswax and worked leather scent the warm workshop while waxed thread rasps through a row of fresh holes. Small hammers tap against wooden lasts as loose buckles and eyelets chime in a shallow tray.

Discovery: A boot awaiting repair has a heel deliberately built around a slim brass key, visible where the worn leather has split.

Setting: Village, Town, City. Condition: Maintained.
