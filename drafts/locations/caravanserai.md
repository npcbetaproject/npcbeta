# Caravanserai

Category: Travel & Trade

Description: A walled roadside lodging and trading compound where caravans rest, stable pack animals, store cargo and exchange news.

Read aloud: A broad courtyard opens beyond a heavy timber gate, with loaded wagons resting beneath shaded stone arcades and pack animals tethered beside a water trough.

More flavour: Harness buckles clink as stablehands lead tired animals toward fresh hay. The air carries the smell of dust, warm bread and cooking spices beneath the murmur of bargaining travelers.

Discovery: A loose stone beside the watering trough conceals a folded cargo receipt with several crate numbers crossed out and replaced in different handwriting.

Setting: Town, City, Wilderness. Condition: Maintained.
