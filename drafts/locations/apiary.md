# Apiary

Category: Rural & Wilderness

Description: A working yard where beekeepers tend straw skeps and timber hives, press honeycomb and prepare honey and wax for trade.

Read aloud: Rows of woven skeps and timber hives stand among wildflowers beside an open shelter crowded with honey jars and a wooden press.

More flavour: Bees drift between the blossoms in a steady golden haze, their hum rising and falling with the breeze. Warm wax and crushed herbs scent the air while honey glistens in shallow bowls beneath the shelter.

Discovery: A hive at the edge of the yard has been built around a small stone coffer; fresh wax seals its lid, but the bees leave the narrow seam untouched.

Setting: Village, Wilderness. Condition: Maintained.
